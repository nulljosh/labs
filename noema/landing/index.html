The name

Noesis is the thinking. Noema is the thought.

Husserl split every act of thought in two: the thinking itself, and the thing as it is thought. This lab does the thinking in public. What survives the checks is the noema.

Reproduce first

Earn trust before chasing anything new

Every field starts by matching a result someone already published. A match counts as a win, and it becomes a test.

The ledger

Every claim, its check and its source

One row per result. Observed, written and machine-checked stay apart, and a finite check never becomes a universal claim.

Every field

Math, computer science, physics, chemistry, medicine, statistics and biology each run the same loop: one exact question, a fixed bound, a second check.

Most results reproduce known work. The first candidate for something new: an exact, proved formula linking frustrated magnets to Wendt's determinant. It is still being checked against the literature.

The loop

Show your work

One

Ask one exact question

A fixed bound and a clear stop. No open-ended searching.

Two

Check it a second way

Brute force, a closed formula or the primary source. Two methods must agree.

Three

Search before calling it new

A match in the literature means reproduced, which still counts as a win.

Four

Write it in the ledger

Claim, check and source in one row, so no page can say more than the ledger holds.
