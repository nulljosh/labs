## First comment

Hi, I'm Josh, I built Doorstock.

I made this for a real garage door shop that needed to know two things fast: what's on the truck, and what's booked this week. Every field-service platform I looked at wanted a subscription and a login just to answer that. Doorstock is the opposite: no account, no sign-in, no network calls at all. Everything you enter lives on your device.

It tracks parts by category (springs, rollers, hinges, openers, weather seal) with a low-stock threshold per part, so reordering is one glance instead of a truck walkthrough. Jobs move through a simple pipeline, Lead, Quote, Scheduled, Complete, Paid, with a customer and address attached to each. The dashboard puts stock count, low-stock warnings, and open job totals on one screen for a ten-second morning check. It ships seeded with industry-standard part types so day one isn't an empty list.

Built for garage-door techs first, but it fits any small trade crew running parts and jobs off a truck. Mac is live on the Mac App Store now, iOS was rejected in an App Review sweep and is under appeal.

Free.
