Quip Sans

Motion and voice

  • The poster wall drifts, and freezes for the speed round so it never fights the clock.
  • A right answer throws confetti. Every fifth in a row throws more. A new high score throws the most.
  • The streak flame bounces when it grows. Quip bobs. All of it stops under reduced motion.
  • Short and a little cocky: “5 in a row!”, “Nope, it was Titanic”. No emojis.

Press

Coda shows you a line from a film, show, game, song or book, and you name it. It plays in the browser, on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Apple TV and Vision Pro, in twenty languages. 1,697 lines, most checked word for word against a published source. A live show every Sunday, a daily quote, duels and weekly cups. Free. Made by Joshua Trommel in Vancouver; his dad had the idea.
