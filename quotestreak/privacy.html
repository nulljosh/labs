Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Without an account

You can play everything without signing in. Your high score, badges, stats, season medals and settings stay on your device. When you answer, the game sends the quote's id and whether you got it right, with nothing that identifies you, so difficulty can be ranked from real answers. There are no analytics, no ads and no tracking scripts.

With an account

An account is optional. It stores your email address (or the Apple or Google account you sign in with), your display name, your scores, your friends and your club, on Cloudflare at api.jaybulb.com. That is what puts you on leaderboards, friends boards, the weekly cup and the season ladder. Delete your account from the account screen and all of it is removed.

Live duels

A duel passes your scores and display name between the two players and anyone watching, through Cloudflare. Nothing is kept once both players leave.

Voice

Both voice settings are off until you turn them on. Reading quotes aloud uses your device's own voice and sends nothing. Answering by voice uses your browser's speech recognition: Safari recognizes speech on your device, while Chrome sends the audio to Google to turn it into text.

Third parties

The site is served by Cloudflare, which may log standard request data such as IP address and browser. The web version loads poster and album art from TMDB and Apple, and opening a story card asks Wikipedia for that title's article. The apps bundle the quotes and use no artwork.
