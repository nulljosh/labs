First comment (maker story):
This started as a joke. The Strait of Hormuz never closes, so I made a site that always said open.

Then in February 2026 it closed, and the joke was wrong. So the site had to stop guessing. Now it counts ships and tells the truth.

The open or closed answer comes from IMF PortWatch daily transit counts, not the headline. Ship counts run about a week behind, and oil is delayed. The page says so.

The page also has a live map of the strait, the WTI crude price with daily, monthly and yearly charts, and a long or short momentum read off the chart you're looking at.

The explainer at the bottom gets denser the further you scroll, like an eye chart. The short answer is at the top. The explainer argues that insurance did the closing.

It's free, with no account. It's not financial advice. Questions go in the comments.
