Reading the trend.

  • It always stayed open. For forty years Iran threatened to close it and never did. Oil spiked on the headline and gave it back within days.
  • Then it shut. On February 28, 2026, after U.S. and Israeli airstrikes, traffic fell from about 85 ships a day to a handful. A June deal reopened it for a few weeks, then it collapsed.
  • Insurance did the closing. War-risk premiums ran to about forty times normal and carriers left. When ships move again, the fear premium comes out of oil.
