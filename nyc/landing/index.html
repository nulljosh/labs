What you do

Build the block. Keep it alive.

Buildings burn materials, and survivors get hungry, tired and stressed. Keep the numbers up and the colony holds.

Build

Six kinds of building

Shelters, food stalls, generators, filter stations, subway access and billboards.

Keep alive

Five numbers to watch

Hunger, oxygen, stress, sleep and health. Let one slide and someone pays for it.

Nights

Raids come at night

Have shelters up and food stocked before the raids hit.

Your orders

Nobody moves until you say so.

Colonists only move when you tell them to. Select one, then tap where it should go. Nothing runs on autopilot.
