Doorstock

Inventory and jobs for garage-door pros. Know what is on the truck and what is booked this week, without running a whole field-service platform to find out.

Mac App Store Open the dashboard

Built for a truck, not a boardroom

Parts by category

Springs, rollers, hinges, openers, seals. Quick quantity controls, so counting stock takes seconds.

Low-stock thresholds

Set a floor per part and see everything that needs reordering at a glance.

A jobs pipeline

Lead, Quote, Scheduled, Complete, Paid. Five stages, nothing to configure.

Seeded and editable

Starts from a list of industry-standard part types, then becomes your own list as you edit it.

Offline by design

No account, no sign-in, no network requests. Everything stays on your device.

Also in the browser

The same inventory and pipeline run at the web dashboard when you are back at the desk.

From a real shop

Doorstock started as the back office for a garage-door business. That side of it is still running at bcgd.jaybulb.com — the booking, pricing and service pages the app keeps stocked.
