Doorstock

Inventory and jobs for garage-door pros.

Doorstock is a small, offline tool for garage-door installers, repair techs, and other small trade contractors who need to know what is on the truck and what is booked this week — without running a whole field-service platform to find out.

What it does

  • Track parts by category — springs, rollers, hinges, openers, seals — with quick quantity controls
  • Set a low-stock threshold per part and see what needs reordering at a glance
  • Run jobs through a simple pipeline: Lead, Quote, Scheduled, Complete, Paid
  • Start from a seeded list of industry-standard part types, then edit it into your own

Get it

Doorstock is on the Mac App Store, where it is listed as BC Garage Doors App — named for the shop it was built to run.

On the web

The same inventory and job pipeline runs in a browser at bcgd-dashboard.jaybulb.com. Accounts there are set up by hand, so it asks you to sign in.

The shop

Doorstock started as the back office for a real garage-door business. That side of it is bcgd.jaybulb.com — the booking, pricing and service pages the app quietly keeps stocked.

Privacy

There is no account and no sign-in. Doorstock makes no network requests and collects no data. Everything you enter is stored locally on your own device, and nothing is transmitted anywhere.

Support
