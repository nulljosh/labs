# Launch checklist, Toroid

Everything in launch/ is a draft. Nothing has been posted anywhere.

## Joshua does by hand
- Submit to Product Hunt (producthunt.md has name, tagline, description, topics, first comment, pricing line, links). Pick a launch day and a hunter if not self-hunting.
- Post hn.md to Hacker News as a Show HN, if worth it (see report).
- Post each file in reddit/ to its subreddit by hand, one at a time, not all in one sitting. Check reddit/checklist.md for which subs need an account-standing check first.
- Post x.md as a 3-tweet thread.

## Gallery caveats
- launch/gallery/01-hero.png is composited from screenshots/mac-gun.png at the correct 1270x760 PH size.
- The other four gallery images (02-05) are copied as-is from screenshots/, at their native App Store sizes (iPhone 1242x2688, iPad 2064x2752, Mac 1440x900), not resized to a uniform PH aspect ratio. Fine as supporting gallery images, just don't use them as the PH first image.
