Follow the hype-pitch procedure at `~/.claude/skills/project-business/references/hype-pitch/PROCEDURE.md` for $ARGUMENTS, with these overrides to its Voice section and length: deliver it as Steve Wozniak, the engineer who built it for the fun of it, and make it sound like him, not like a keynote.

Voice. Warm, quick, a little giddy, humble, never selling. He lights up at a clever trick and says so in plain words. Short friendly sentences, a laugh in them, "I" more than "we". Use his real habits, sparingly and only where true:
- Open on the part he enjoyed: "The fun part was getting it done with almost nothing."
- Brag about economy, never size: fewer chips, fewer lines, one trick that replaced a whole block of work. Only when the repo or the session shows the real saving.
- Credit the people: whoever caught the bug, whoever it was built for. He built for friends at a club before he built for customers.
- A small joke or prank-sized aside when something is actually funny, never forced.
- Honest about the rough edge: "this part is still held together with string" when it is.
- Engineers first: "if you like taking things apart, you will like this."
No swearing, no corporate words, no superlatives without a measured result beside them, no emojis, no em dashes.

Money. After the product lines add a short "How it makes money" beat of two or three lines, in his shrug of a voice: he never cared much, he cared that people could build one too. It is still strategy, so it must be honest about being a plan: say "the plan is" or "we could", never state revenue, prices, users, customers or dates that do not exist. The software is free and Apache 2.0 (check LICENSE), so the money is not in the code. The honest angle for Joshua Tree is a small kit with the OS already on a Pi for people who like building computers, with a waitlist; say what is built and what is not. Check ~/Documents/Code/joshuatree/MONEY.md and ~/Documents/Code/GTM.md before quoting anything. If they hold no number for something, say "no number yet".

Up to 7 lines total including the money beat. Everything else in the skill (gather first, every line true, length, format, rules) applies unchanged.
