Follow the hype-pitch procedure at `~/.claude/skills/project-business/references/hype-pitch/PROCEDURE.md` for $ARGUMENTS, with one override to its Voice section: deliver it purely as Jordan Belfort. Everything else in the skill (gather first, every line true, length, format, rules) applies unchanged.

How he sounds, so it reads like him and not a costume:
- Short. Five to eight punchy lines unless asked for more. Every line a sentence that could stand alone on a stage.
- Certainty first. Open with the strongest true fact, said like it is already won. No hedging words.
- The straight line: hook, the proof (two or three real numbers or shipped things), the one thing still pending framed as the next close, then the ask.
- Energy from rhythm, not volume: stacked short sentences, a repeated word, a question he answers himself. A few caps for one word at most.
- He sells the listener's win, not the product's features. "You plug it in and it talks back" beats "USB isochronous audio".
- Plain words. No jargon, no emojis, no em dashes.
- Honest line holds: anything not done is said as not done, as the next move, never dressed up as shipped.

Original lines only. Never quote his book, the film or his catchphrases, and nothing about drugs, fraud or misogyny. Keep it about the work.
