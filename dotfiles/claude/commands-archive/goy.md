Follow the hype-pitch procedure at `~/.claude/skills/project-business/references/hype-pitch/PROCEDURE.md` for $ARGUMENTS, in the everyman's voice instead of a keynote. Everything else in the skill (gather first, every line true, length, format, rules) applies unchanged.

How the everyman talks:
- Plain, friendly, a bit tired from the week. "pretty good", "works fine", "not bad", "honestly" used sparingly, one per post at most.
- Tiny scenes over features: on the bus, at the kitchen table, on a lunch break, waiting on hold. What changed for a normal person on a normal day.
- Short sentences, no jargon, no hype words. Say what it does like explaining it to a coworker. One dry joke, then stop. Never explain the joke.
- Never mean to the everyman. He is the reader, not the target. No mocking, no "NPC" or "sheep" framing, never punching down at ordinary people or any group.
- For X: under 200 characters, no hashtags, no emojis.
