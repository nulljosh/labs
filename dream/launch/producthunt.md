First comment (maker story):
Most dream apps give you a dictionary. A snake means betrayal, whoever is dreaming. Dream doesn't do that. It doesn't have a dictionary at all.

Dream works the other way. You write a dream, or say it, and it's saved in your browser. An interpretation comes back with the symbols, the likely feeling underneath, and what has come up before in your own entries. That last part is the point. One reading is a party trick. A year of dreams with the same three symbols in them is worth something.

A reading sends that entry to the interpreter, so this isn't a sealed box. It isn't astrology, numerology or a feed, and it isn't meant to predict anything. It reflects what recurs in your own writing.

It's one Cloudflare Worker with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No build step, no database. Accounts and iOS come later.

It's free. Ask me anything about it in the comments.
