Everything that tells time

One page, every clock

The clock you reach for ten times a day, without the ads, the account or the app store.

World clocks

Every city you care about

Add a city and see its time, whether the sun is up there, and how far ahead it is. Slide the planner to find an hour that suits everyone.

Alarms

Alarms and a bedtime

Repeat on the days you pick, skip one when you sleep in, and wake to a sound that fades in. Set a bedtime and Horula counts your sleep.

Stopwatch and timers

Run as many as you need

A stopwatch with laps. Timers that run side by side and remember the ones you use most.

Nightstand

A bedside clock

Big digits that dim at night. The screen stays on until you tap it.

Offline

Private
