# Brand brief

Source: bcgaragedoors.ca (home, services, tips, specials, reviews pages) and Google Maps, checked 2026-10-11. Use this as the project knowledge for any Claude Project.

**Who:** Best Choice Garage Door Services. Family-owned, third generation, 30+ years, based in Langley BC, serving the Lower Mainland. Open 24 hours, 7 days. (604) 240-0180, bcgaragedoors.ca, Instagram @bcgaragedoorsofficial. Brian is the face of the business.
**What:** Repairs, not new doors. Springs, cables, rollers, tracks, panels, hinges, keypads, clickers, weather strips, maintenance, emergency repair. They do not sell or install new doors.
**Customer:** a homeowner with a broken or stuck door who wants it fixed fast and honestly.
**Tone:** honest, local, plain, a little warm and funny. No pressure. Brian answers Google reviews with jokes and emoji.
**Selling points:** repair first, no upsell. Same-day and emergency service. Own team, no subcontractors. LiftMaster-compatible parts. Plain-language explanation before any work. A free garage door safety check.
**Proof:** 4.9 stars from 217 Google reviews. Six reviews on the site. A truck wrapped in the "Attack of the Garage Door" art.
**Gaps:** no job gallery, no before and after photos, homepage video from 2017, no prices or warranty terms stated. Never invent any of these.
**Colors and type:** teal #1B5959, dark teal #134040, charcoal #3A3E40, sage #8DA6A6, orange #F47216 for call buttons. Barlow Condensed headlines in caps, Open Sans body.
**Phrases he already uses:** "We fix it, we don't upsell." "Three generations." "Call anytime." "Honest advice." "Repair Specialist."
