September opened with security holes closed across six apps, Epiphany's stale broker key rotated with self-healing added, and Nimble's live app preview wired into landing pages. I built Plain, an iOS and macOS text editor with syntax highlighting and Ollama completions, and Breathe, a breathing patterns app, in a day. Next day I set up Ollama and did the native rollout: all 17 apps got real Kotlin ports, Swing got pulled out on its own, accessibility checks passed, seven apps got an icon pass, and the fleet hit 30 apps live. I fixed Wordroot's styles leaking in from the chemistry templates and its stuck theme color, started Slicehack, updated the portfolio, and closed a hole in Epiphany's webhook that let one account reach another's data.

That Friday I fixed Epiphany's macOS build, broken by old leftovers, and its map mirroring. I built a skill that hands repo chores to a local model, which caught drift bugs in Sidewise and Roost; llama3.1 went in for better tool calling. Talli got a legal aid directory and notifications, and the Duolingo chess bot paused on an iframe blocker. Saturday I started Hackrange, a security dojo, renamed Curbside to Curbfind, and built 16 terminal apps across the fleet. I published the Plan repo and Bookrank's Listen feature, finished Apple sign-in on Litigate and Lexly, fixed Epiphany's flights map, and standardized 42 repos. Doorstock got ready for App Review, Fieldbook grew to 92 fields with virology, paleontology, and robotics, and Homeqi's web assessment got ported to native iOS, unsubmitted while an appeal is pending.

Sunday I wired Stripe into Healstack and Sparkjar, published Seamark on npm, and shipped Weather, Fieldbook, and Lucarne, a small browser whose landing page is the app itself. The new landing demo skill put the real app in a live device frame on sixteen app pages. I fixed Epiphany's web purchase, switched to Interactive Brokers for Canadian investing, and sent Lucarne and Plain to review. Monday I caught Epiphany's broker sync looping and burning 480 thousand Workers requests since Friday; one line came out and it stopped. I added a lost-cat listing to Homeward for Vinney, an orange tabby last seen in Brookswood, Langley.

On the 8th I flipped five repos to public, finished icon fixes on Lucarne and Plain, and got my CRA Business Number, submitted to App Store Connect to unblock iOS revenue. The Duolingo breakthrough came the same day: the correct moves sit in the page's own state, so no board scanning, just reading and clicking, and the language solver reads answer pairs the same way. I bundled a chess engine, found the live board position, and beat the bot in game one, unlocking the next league rank. The Greek course then ran unattended through dozens of lessons, its ledger past a thousand entries, once a timeout fix let it resume from checkpoint. The chess work went into Seamark as reusable pieces, and the solver's page went up at pwnlingo.heyitsmejosh.com.

Overnight the loop ran across Zulu, Portuguese, and Romanian, with a chess runner clicking through 1,700 puzzles with zero misses, despite some lost bot games. I rewrote the engine as one four-hundred-line runner replacing three loops, a Chrome extension, and a file server, and pruned a hundred dead wrapper layers, taking the code from 21.6k lines to 19k. Epiphany iOS got approved that morning with paid Daily Brief live, plus a synced splash icon and prefetched Markets news. I built Co-Stanza, a poetry network with iOS, macOS, and Kotlin ports, and fixed Voxprint's landing page, Tripwire's request limits, a Curbfind redirect loop, and Sparkjar's iOS comment posting and pagination. The Pwnlingo status app got a rename, icon buttons instead of a track dropdown, lesson counts, a misses counter, and a live track switch.

On the 10th the paid apps went out: Voxprint at one dollar, NYC with icons, and Epiphany Premium as an in-app purchase. I built the launch skill for Product Hunt across 12 apps, made launch kits for Litigate, Sparkjar, Blockframe, and Doorstock, and scheduled thirteen apps starting the 11th. Wordroot's macOS appeal won, Curbfind got approved, and Windgate, the renamed Breathe, shipped to the App Store. The Duolingo solver became a native iOS and macOS app with a hardened runner, crash fixes, and watchdog timers. Blockframe got canvas editing, 16 landing pages lost their redundant headings, and an Epiphany news drawer bug doubling wait times past the 15 second iOS timeout got fixed. I tried Codex for the first time: Roost lazy-loads its routes, Fieldbook got a related-fields sidebar and PDF printing, Weather shows hourly forecasts, and Pwnlingo gained full translations, auto-advance, and uptime tracking. Apple rejected one late submission on an automated check, no reason given.

Friday the 11th I fixed Pwnlingo's auto-start, language menu, crash recovery, and tunnel detection, and made the tunnel heal itself. I verified Cloudflare Web Analytics, wired social sign-in to nine apps, and polished landing pages across twelve repos with project graphs, hero trims, a move from Vercel to Workers, and security header fixes. The mail skill became its own app, Siftbox, with Gmail, Outlook, and iCloud sign-in, submitted for iOS and macOS. Every repo got baseline docs, and Tripwire learned to fix things itself with Workers AI. Conveyer got a real Factorio client spectating, a text interface, progress graphs from 20 runs, and a full-screen video hero in terracotta. SketchyBar now shows Pwnlingo's status, and I added watch apps to Windgate, Numen, and Sidewise, and locked Doorstock behind Face ID with Apple and Google sign-in; ten other apps stayed off the watch, since browsing and reading don't fit a 40mm screen.

Saturday the 12th I polished Conveyer's video, triaged Notes into roadmaps, cleared Product Hunt spam, and gave SketchyBar a Claude usage indicator. I fixed Healstack's Face ID persistence, Epiphany's Markets banner and landing geolocation, Quotestreak's accent colors, Inkpress's article view and caching, Sparkjar's icon, and Voxprint's Pro flow. Tripwire now picks its watch list from GitHub and writes patch PRs from failing CI logs, and I committed the missing fixtures breaking Talli's tests, then submitted Talli for review. Pwnlingo's crash loop was a detached process, so it restarts itself now; later its tunnel drifted to Korean and the watchdog stalled two and a half hours, still unexplained. Wordroot's shipped app was missing its photo backgrounds, so I resubmitted it and added a Mac shipping workflow. I renamed Jarvis to Gato with a live routing demo, cut Epiphany's instructions file from 630 lines to 32, and built the PR investigation and GitHub feed skills. On four MonoCode PRs the review skill found a version gate with the wrong minimum, fixed with boundary tests. Swing's silent camera failure got fixed and two automated callers connected, with a real-device call and a network relay still open. Epiphany gained nearby places with category chips. Plain became a small IDE on the Mac with a file browser, chat, output, and terminal panes, plus formatting on save. I built the calendar skill, refreshed Sparkjar's five screenshots, and closed with a wrapup across 50 repos and 180 commits. Cadence's landing page lost its yellow and its fake native-app section, and Lexly's lessons now explain the answer after you respond, piloted on French's first unit.
