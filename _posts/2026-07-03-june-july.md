Epiphany finally shipped to the App Store this month, closing out a long stretch of revisions. Half the portfolio got renamed in the process: Dose became Healstack, Lingo became Lexly, Books became Spine, and Brief became Casewright, each rename dragging a branding and icon pass behind it.

Reconnected with Jayda for the first time in about ten years, three days together with a couple of drives out to catch the sunset. Good, but mostly a reminder of how much I value my own quiet now. School plans changed too: computer science at UVIC instead of the paralegal route.

## Saturday

Fixed a batch of Epiphany bugs, the good one being why the autopilot did nothing in testing. It was rounding share counts down to whole numbers, so every order under about a hundred and fifty dollars silently became an order for zero shares. Now it handles fractions.

## Sunday

Found the net worth number was quietly wrong. It ignored uninvested cash sitting in investment accounts, so it showed two hundred and twenty nine dollars when the accounts actually held four hundred and fifteen. Also smoothed out the markets drawer, which was choppy because my animation was fighting the drag gesture.

## Monday

Inkpress was loading blank entries because the feed parser choked on wrapped text. Renamed it from Journal along the way, since colliding with Apple's own app was never going to end well. Wired one-command release pipelines across all eight shipping apps so a version can go out without leaving the terminal.

## Tuesday

Talli's Messages tab was blank, and the cause was the parser silently dropping any message that did not put its date on its own line. Spent the rest of the night on app icons and hit the wall properly: five apps could not upload because their distribution profiles did not exist yet.

## Wednesday

Chased the provisioning blocker down to a single missing piece of configuration that only exists in Apple's web portal with no API path. Linked it, regenerated the profiles, and it still came back empty twice. Genuinely stuck rather than nearly done, which is worth writing down honestly.

## Thursday

Rolled a shared body font across the smaller sites so they stop being monospace everywhere. Spark's startup-ideas feature went cross platform, web and phone and desktop all showing the same thing.

## Friday

Found out only four of eleven apps were showing up in TestFlight. I had added myself as an email-invited tester, which needs a manual accept per app, rather than as a team member who appears everywhere automatically. Folded five small experiments into one repo and archived the originals, which took the repo count from twenty down to fifteen.

## Apps

Epiphany live. Healstack, Lexly, Casewright and Inkpress all in review or close to it. Spark still blocked on Apple's portal.
