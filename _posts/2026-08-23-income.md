## Sunday (August 23)

Fixed the Talli web home tab, which has been showing the wrong monthly income amount since May. The scraper read the provincial PWD half from My Self Serve, but the federal Canada Disability Benefit never appears there. Switched to reading from the derived config instead, so the number's now right at $1,650. Also replaced the hardcoded payment history with dynamic derivation from the schedule, so the recent-payments chart and year-to-date total self-update. The 2026 sum jumped from $5,300 to $11,550.
