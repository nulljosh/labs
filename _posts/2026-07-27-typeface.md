## Sunday

Ripped Google Fonts out of a handful of apps and went back to whatever font the machine already has. Pages feel instant now, and I keep wondering why I ever paid a round trip for something the OS ships for free. Also fixed two Epiphany bugs I'd been ignoring: the Flights panel had a units mistake that was asking for twice the search area it should, and the saved-statements screen had a callback rebuilding itself in an endless loop.

## Monday

Went to open-source the litigation app and found something much worse instead: the live site was serving real court documents to anyone, unauthenticated. Dropped everything and fixed it, moving the documents behind a real auth gate instead of a CSS overlay that never actually protected anything. Also gave Lexly's icon a proper redesign since the old one was invisible outline strokes, and got BCGD's booking form actually saving leads to a database instead of throwing them away.

## Tuesday to Wednesday

Found an old personal page ranking on Google for my name, a private timeline I'd written during therapy that never should have stayed public. Pulled it down immediately. Spent most of the rest of the stretch finishing book summaries (IBS, Sobriety, Statistics, and Good Feng Shui all done now) and closing out App Store rejections that had been sitting for weeks. Renamed Spine to Uprighty and Grapher to Curvely since both names had drifted from what the App Store actually let me register. Redesigned Epiphany's icon after two failed attempts (accidentally teal, then accidentally purple) before landing on a lime-green network on near-black. Fixed two stuck Mac review submissions by learning that Apple's resubmit command silently reuses the most recent submission regardless of its state, so a broken one needs cancelling first.

Also learned the hard way not to fire off six background agents at once. Usage jumped from under half to over ninety percent in about ten minutes and four of them died before finishing anything. One at a time from now on.

## Apps

Talli 3.5.12 shipped. Uprighty submitted for review with all its book summaries done. Lexly iOS and Sparkjar Mac both unblocked and back in review. Litigate's data leak closed for good. Wiretext and Wordroot got their first working App Store builds.
