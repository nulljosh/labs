## Sunday

Ripped Google Fonts out of Echo, Spine, Sparkjar and Talli and went back to whatever font the machine already has. Pages feel instant now, and I keep wondering why I ever paid a round trip for something the OS ships for free. Along the way I found out Echo's landing page isn't on Vercel at all, it's on Cloudflare Pages with no auto-deploy, so every push needs a manual wrangler command. That explained a few "why didn't my change go live" moments.

Also went back and fixed two Epiphany bugs I'd been ignoring. The Flights panel had never worked, and it turned out to be a units mistake: a radius being treated as a full span, so every request asked for twice the area the API allows. Same mistake in the iOS and Mac clients. The saved statements error was a loop, a callback getting rebuilt on every change and refetching forever. Both fixed. Put the People tab and Daily Brief behind Pro too, since they'd been quietly free.

Small stuff: Spine has a To Read list now, Talli's icon is blue and 3.5.11 is off to review, and I finally got the blue circle off my portfolio avatar by flood-filling from the border instead of color-keying, which kept the eyes intact.

## Monday

Tackled BCGD's booking form, which had been throwing away every lead it received. Built a real database pipeline and added service/area pages so the site is now an actual site, not a page pretending to be one. Then I went to open-source the litigation app and found something much worse: the live site was serving real court documents to unauthenticated users. Logged it as the top priority and stopped.

Fixed Lexly's app icon too—the old one was outline strokes that were invisible at any size, so I redesigned it to a solid white speech bubble with blue dots for legibility. Also stripped out the serif display font and Google Fonts links that made the landing page look decorative. Screenshots were stale (still showing the pre-rename "Lingo" title), so I re-shot them and created a new iOS v1.1.3 to hold the corrected assets. macOS v1.1.1 screenshot also fixed.

## Apps

Talli 3.5.11 submitted. Echo 1.3.4 in review. Spine and Sparkjar web updated. Lexly icon redesigned, iOS v1.1.3 and macOS v1.1.1 screenshots fixed (awaiting builds). BCGD live with a working leads pipeline. Litigate blocked on the auth leak.
