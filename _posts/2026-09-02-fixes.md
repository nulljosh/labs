Spent the evening closing holes across six apps. Talli had two auth issues. one dev-credential fallback was checking a Vercel environment variable that never existed on Cloudflare Workers; the other endpoint was serving private pages to anyone. Both are gated now. Swing got text chat on the lobby socket. Quotestreak's art library was 45 percent full; I backfilled 37 music covers via iTunes lookup and verified all 188 URLs. Curvely's color picker crashed on edge cases. Healstack's floating tab bar was in a ZStack overlay, so every sheet had to clear it manually. I moved it to a `.safeAreaInset` on the TabView so the safe area handles it everywhere. The real news is Curbside finally looks like an app. I built an icon, wired it on Mac and iOS, swept the Mac UX, and deployed web icons.

Epiphany's broker sync had been silently dead in production. The SnapTrade consumer key on the Worker was wrong from the August Vercel migration. I rotated it, added self-healing re-registration, and pulled transaction history so holdings show real cost basis, dividends, and recent activity.

The only wall was the privacy policy. Bookrank and Litigate had App Store privacy URLs waiting, but Apple rejects the PATCH while an app-info is in READY_FOR_DISTRIBUTION state.

Wordroot's landing showed two App Store banners. Deleted mine.

Nimble's landing demo is the iPhone frame (dropped the Linux card, made the footer bulb yellow). Mac still in review.

Apps summary: Six-app sprint complete (Talli, Swing, Quotestreak, Curvely, Healstack, Curbside). Epiphany broker sync fixed, cost basis live.
