## Sunday (2026-08-31)

Three apps got API and MCP endpoints: Wordroot, Quotestreak, and Bookrank moved to Cloudflare Pages for server-side Functions, and Quotestreak and Bookrank migrated their DNS from GitHub Pages. Found eight forgotten commits in curbside and set up its first GitHub remote, then updated the codebase notes with the last month's repos (homeward, roost, numen, swing, curbside) and rewrote cross-platform coverage to center on Kotlin Multiplatform Compose.

Spent the evening scanning for algorithmic opportunities. Game of Life's toroidal wrap now resolves once per row instead of twice per neighbor, dropping a 400x400 board simulation from 600ms to 136ms, and I added whitepaper notes on adaptive sampling for the grapher, spatial partitioning for the city sim, and clustering for the news aggregator.
