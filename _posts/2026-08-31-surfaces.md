## Monday (2026-08-31)

Brought Quotestreak's iOS and macOS apps up to parity with the web game and sent both to review, rewrote the portfolio homepage to lead with the Talli story (tracking disability benefits meant understanding paperwork nobody explains) and added pricing clarity, wrapped API and MCP endpoints on five more apps, and refreshed App Store screenshots for Curvely and Charwork. The Mac was at 90% capacity with disk-full alerts, so I ran cleanup and freed 36 gigabytes (DerivedData 14 GB, build caches and Homebrew another 5 GB combined). One useful finding: simulator storage reports 44 GB but that's inflated by APFS clones - erasing every simulator only freed 1 GB, so DerivedData was the real disk hog all along.
