## Sunday (2026-08-31)

Added API and MCP surfaces to three apps, extracted curbside, rewrote cross-platform notes for Kotlin, built brain as a RAG system indexing 777 wiki chunks, wrapped the share feature across 26 apps, and fixed the Sidewise CI by unquoting a shell glob. Audited monetization and found Stripe was half-dead: only Talli and Epiphany can actually charge, because Healstack and Sparkjar never got a live key. Epiphany's webhook silently failed after the Cloudflare migration, leaving every paid purchase charging the card but not unlocking the paid tier. Fixed it by carrying the signed bytes as rawBody and verifying with constructEventAsync, added the same handler to Healstack, but skipped its paywall since it earns nothing.
