## Chess

Elon Musk posted that the number of moves in chess that are not utterly stupid is tiny, and that chess will one day be fully solved like checkers. Chess.com quote tweeted it with "skill issue" and the replies turned into a fight about whether chess is a puzzle or a game.

Checkers really was solved, which is why the analogy breaks. Checkers has about a hundred billion billion positions, small enough to store. Chess has more legal positions than there are atoms in the observable universe. Solved means handing over the whole answer, and nothing in this universe is big enough to hold it. Better play forever, yes. Solved, no.

Good frame for what I am building. The Duolingo bot already grinds language lessons unattended, and Duolingo added chess, so it is becoming a self learning chess and language player. A language lesson has one right answer to memorize. Chess does not, so the bot has to judge positions and keep what worked. Last week's blocker stands: the board sits in a cross-origin iframe, so every move goes through screenshots and clicks.
