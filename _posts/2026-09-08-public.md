## Chess

Chess.com and Elon Musk spent the day arguing on Twitter about whether chess is a puzzle or a game, and whether it can be solved. It cannot. There are more legal chess positions than atoms in the observable universe, so nobody is ever building that lookup table. Solved means you can hand someone the whole answer, and chess is too big to hand over.

Good frame for what I am building. The Duolingo bot already grinds language lessons unattended, and Duolingo added chess, so it is turning into a self learning chess and language player. A language lesson has one right answer to memorize. Chess does not, so the bot has to judge positions and keep what worked. Last week's blocker still stands: the board sits in a cross-origin iframe, so nothing reads the DOM and every move goes through screenshots and clicks.
