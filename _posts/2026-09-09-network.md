## Tuesday

Built and shipped Co-Stanza, a poetry network shaped like Svbtle: one column, your name, your poems, nothing else. It's live at co-stanza.heyitsmejosh.com with iOS, macOS and Kotlin ports all talking to a shared Supabase project through row-level security. Sign in, register, forgot and reset password work everywhere, and I added an authmail theme for Co-Stanza. Cleaned up the voxprint repo too: dropped the dead web PWA, removed unused ExportOptions plists, and trimmed the git history, shrinking it from 1.5 GB to 518 MB. Also fixed tripwire's supabase-spark-db watch: the 6-hour cron was hitting Workers subrequest limits, so I capped the code-search calls with a shared budget to let it handle wide-table specs. Debugged a funny Curbfind bug where the landing page's CTA button and hero iframe both linked to `/app` without a trailing slash. Cloudflare Pages 404s on `/app` and falls back to serving the landing page's own index.html, so clicking the button just reloaded the same page and looked like it redirected to itself. Fixed by adding the trailing slash.

## Wednesday

Got Paid Apps Agreement approved, so shipped Voxprint with a $1 paywall and NYC Survive with fixed icons, and built Epiphany's Premium tier via StoreKit 2, all three declaring exempt encryption. Built a `/launch` skill that generates Product Hunt kits and ran it across 12 apps, with Voxprint's post going live Sep 10 and the other 11 apps' kits staged for later. NYC Survive iOS got approved on the App Store; I added the App Store link, rebuilt, and deployed to Cloudflare. Tested the paid pricing on nine apps at $0.99 briefly but reverted them to free by day's end, keeping the three-app focus strategy.
