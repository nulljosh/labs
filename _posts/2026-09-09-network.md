## Tuesday

Built and shipped Co-Stanza, a Svbtle-shaped poetry network with iOS, macOS and Kotlin ports all using Supabase with row-level security and full auth. Cleaned up voxprint's repo (shrinking it from 1.5 GB to 518 MB) and fixed tripwire's subrequest limit issues. Tested the paid pricing on nine apps at $0.99 briefly but reverted them to free by day's end, keeping the three-app focus strategy.
