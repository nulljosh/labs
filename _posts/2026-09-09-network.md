## Tuesday

Built and shipped Co-Stanza, a poetry network shaped like Svbtle: one column, your name, your poems, nothing else. It's live at co-stanza.heyitsmejosh.com with iOS, macOS and Kotlin ports all talking to a shared Supabase project through row-level security. Sign in, register, forgot and reset password work everywhere. Also added an authmail theme for Co-Stanza.
