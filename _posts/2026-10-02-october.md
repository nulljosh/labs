## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree reached 2.0 with twenty-six apps in protected boxes. Bookrank is live on iPhone with natural voices and offline reading. 

Premium is $1 and auto-trading is live. A stock rule I paper-traded, Double 7s, hit 77 percent. Notate is live on iPhone.

## Oct 4

A Pi 4B is coming for hardware tests.

My idea app became Hikko, since Hotaru was taken, and iPhone and Mac are in review.

Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign and a privacy fix; iOS is live, Mac in review.

Turing shipped with file-picking answers, a new Mac icon and her own 1.5B model. Wrong picks fell under 10 per set; refusals fell to 2.6 percent.

## Oct 5

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a refreshed logo on Samantha's face.

Apple rejected seven apps under spam: Hamurapi, Windgate, Madobe, Mailbag, Curbfind, Plaintxt, Sidewise; Hikko got stale screenshots. All five resubmitted.

Epiphany's map search uses Places now; iPhone submitted. Apple rejected Curbfind's update and I replied.

NYC Survive's overnight loop made it winnable; iOS is live, macOS in review. A headless bot wins it in two hours.

Nimble shipped spoken math.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a cache flush fix.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi; the chip has no login helper, so I wrote its WPA2 handshake and the green light blinks again.

Samantha answers on the real Pi using my API credit. My Mac relay picks a cheap or strong model per question and gives her read-only access to a shared folder.

Samantha now prints a note and blinks the Pi light on an agent’s branch. A safety check blocked a Codex mode letting anyone on the network run programs on my Mac. An overnight runner works branches only, $5 cap per run.

Money comes from a Pi dev kit with a waitlist; the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote the Joshua Tree story as an eleven-chapter book with board photos.

The Pi got a scientific calculator and a tiny language model. After a DNS fix it loaded heyitsmejosh.com over HTTPS, its first web page; scripts still don't run.

Every Pi image carried my relay key. That fix is in review, with two more queued: the key crosses Wi-Fi in plain text, and the clock trusts an unchecked date header.

## Oct 9 to 10

I halved my custom skills and flashed the Pi card. Relay encryption and clock checks still need hardware.

## Oct 11

I built xread, a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models. Most web apps moved from Supabase to spark-api, my Cloudflare backend; passwords stayed behind, so everyone resets. Heyitsmejosh.com lapses on the 13th by choice, so the brand moves to jaybulb.com.

The Mac app’s hideous layout became three columns, like Mail. When spark-api stopped recognising the installed apps' sign-in, they fell back to the robot voice; the fix was approved today. Native users used to share one daily voice cap; each gets one.

From chat, Turing can boot, type into, screenshot and stop Joshua Tree, and read its log. A real boot found a bug that could have killed another session's build machine, so she now stops only her own. I tested 109 of her older abilities and fixed 24. Her newest training run became the tool picker, better on four of five test sets with fewer leaks, and the iPhone app runs her tools too.

I started marketing Dad's Langley garage door business: 37 Instagram posts, a $4 trailer. A stock voice sounded wrong, so he records his own.

I cleared 93 Watch Later videos and moved my 69 saved X bookmarks into notes.

Four repos took their new names: Healstack is Intake, Voxprint is Notate, Sparkjar is Hikko and Lexly is Tonchi. Hacker News turned down Notate's Show HN, so I posted fourteen X launch threads. Eleven App Store listings moved to jaybulb.com where Apple allows. A Talli login bug on the new domain was a server allowlist I forgot.

Nimble answers carry photos, news has thumbnails, and answers cite up to three sources. I gave it a typeface, Nimble Sans, and a brand kit. It's with Apple.

Twenty Joshua Tree changes merged; my first Raspberry Pi 4 run showed it boots to a desktop, search works, the beep plays and Calendar opens. Bluetooth finds my mouse, but it hangs up before pairing. Tree Sans is live on the landing page; the OS-wide font is built, not pushed. The 3.0 release stays a draft until the board pass is done.

## Oct 12

Nimble only shows photos that match the answer's subject, credited to Wikipedia. Looping my exam burned the free AI quota. The app now shows a quiet note that the free AI is resting.

Today I worked on Turing’s tool picker, which chooses abilities for each question. It clears all three of my bars on dev, and the copy Joshua Tree carries is small C that stays out of plain chat, firing on 20 of 434 questions that needed no tool. The numbers are tuned to dev, so the sealed sets stay unread.

The Pi stack folded ten pull requests into "the Pi gets every app." I flashed the card and ran it on real hardware. It boots, and the Bluetooth mouse reconnects on its own. Multi-window is still broken, the boot bar is choppy, and the wallpaper is still terracotta. Nimbus hit an A on round seven.

## Oct 13

I cut Joshua Tree’s docs from fifty-six files to eleven; GitHub now says "A computer." The Pi release waits on local test failures an agent is fixing. I approved a plan from many windows at 4.0 to a daily driver at 10.0. On the real 3.9 card, my mouse survives reboot, but dragging redraws the whole screen, causing lag, and only two windows open. Nimbus is on round eight with a print pack for a shop. Nimble got a three-mode privacy page, spoken conversations and Copy on Mac. Turing's picker made 115 false calls against a bar of 22; an abstain step now clears all three dev bars, with the tuning caveat and PR still open. Her iPhone voice answers aloud. With no USB mouse, I re-paired Bluetooth by hand and wrote a Connect script.

I fixed my terminal’s broken shortcut, then tracked Codex’s repeated failures to the Stripe plugin starting without its scripts. All seven hooks pass now; restarting Codex remains the last check, and a plugin update could undo the repair.

I started Evidna on jaybulb.com, checking every one-letter change in a blood-protein gene. Repeatable benchmarks keep calculations separate from experiments.

## Oct 14

I built Noema on jaybulb.com: blue, with hands-on math examples. First-descent checks passed through a million; algebra covers twenty-eight of thirty-two groups, and Lean checked one small proof. Collatz remains unsolved.

I brought my YouTube summary skill into Codex and tried an eighty-lecture discrete math course. The opening lectures are saved in iCloud, with the rest underway.
