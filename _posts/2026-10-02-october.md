## Oct 1 to 2

Joshua Tree reached 2.0. Twenty-six apps run in protected boxes, and the last flaky tests are fixed. Bookrank is live on iPhone with natural voices, Goodreads import, offline reading and highlights. All my apps are now 99 cents.

Epiphany maps load in under 4 seconds, down from 23. Premium plan is $1 and auto-trading is live. A stock rule I tested on paper money, Double 7s, hit 77 percent.

Talli was rejected over a broken test account; fixed and resubmitted. Notate went live on iPhone at 99 cents.

## Oct 3

Joshua Tree shipped six versions with a 30-second intro and Music and Movies apps. I started moving the kernel to a Pi. Lexly resubmitted with Pre-Calc 12.
## Oct 4

A Raspberry Pi 4B is on the way to test Joshua Tree on real hardware. Apps on the Pi run in locked rooms, and Bookrank, Tonchi and Hikko show real data.

My idea-catching app became Hikko (Hotaru was taken). Near-white paper with terracotta accent. iPhone and Mac 3.0 in review; Mac got terracotta buttons and sidebar.

Mailbag is my mail app. Fixed Mac window focus, demo sign-in and the sorter. Blocked: Gmail sign-in on Mac.

Lexly became Tonchi. Content grew to 165 courses. 14 of 32 language versions kept.

Healstack got a web redesign and a privacy fix, and it is becoming Intake. iOS live; Mac in review.

Turing shipped with file-picking answers and new Mac icon, her own 1.5B model (850 MB). Wrong picks under 10 per set. v5.1 cut refusals to 2.6 percent.

## Oct 5

Joshua Tree landing is live with a refreshed logo on Samantha's face.

Apple rejected seven apps under spam: Hamurapi, Windgate, Madobe, Mailbag, Curbfind, Plaintxt, Sidewise; Hikko got stale-screenshots. Plaintxt now has line numbers, Go to Line and preview. Sidewise rebuilt with Compare, Share extension, widget. Madobe iOS and macOS got Mac Float, Pair pages, per-site rules, App Intents. All five resubmitted.
Epiphany's map search now uses Places; iPhone submitted. Curbfind shipped Near me and a smarter ranker; Apple rejected it and I replied. Conveyer retired the beaten world.

NYC Survive's overnight loop made the game winnable and shipped it (iOS live, macOS in review). Recruits tie to beds and food, squads take orders, and night raids come unless you set PATROL. A headless bot plays it to victory in about two hours, and the landing has real playthrough video.
## Oct 5 to 6

Swept 15 Apple Notes into project roadmaps and emptied Notes.app. Nimble shipped spoken math and dropped "Answers" from its store name.

Notate, Talli and Plank got UI fixes, and Joshua Tree added a desktop button on the hero.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after fixing the CPU cache flush. The USB driver worked in QEMU, then on the Pi with a cable swap. A Claude relay lets my Mac build the OS inside it.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi. The chip has no login helper, so I wrote the WPA2 handshake. The green light blinks on the board again.

Samantha answers on the real Pi through my API credit. A relay on my Mac picks a cheap or strong model per question. She can read a shared Mac folder, read-only. 

An agent wrote the Pi-side actions on its branch. Asking Samantha now prints a note and blinks the light. The safety check rightly blocked a Codex mode, since anyone on the network could run programs on my Mac. Codex on the Pi waits for a secure tunnel. An overnight runner works the repo on branches only, $5 cap per run. The API spending limit is now $200.

Next: split the kernel's giant C file, then HTTPS on the Pi. Money comes from a Pi dev kit with a waitlist live, and the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote the Joshua Tree story as an eleven-chapter book with board photos. The Pi's first benchmark: memory copies about 1100 MB/s, Wi-Fi in 6.4 seconds, clock in 8.7.

The Pi got a scientific calculator and a tiny language model. The browser stalled on DNS. After the fix, the Pi fetched heyitsmejosh.com over HTTPS, showing its title and a link. It is the first web page my OS has loaded on the board, but it cannot run scripts.

I also found a real hole. Every Pi image carried my relay key, not just the dev card. The fix is in review. Two more are on the list: the key goes over Wi-Fi in plain text, and the clock trusts an unchecked date header. Helpers spent $133 of the $200 credit, leaving about $66 until Nov 4.

## Oct 9

I halved my custom skills, fixed Pi keyboard paths and flashed the checked card. Signup email arrived; I redesigned it, fixed mobile signup, tightened Samantha's shared-folder access, and wrote the Wi-Fi guide. Relay encryption and clock protection still need hardware checks.

## Oct 10

The landing refresh is live, QA passed. Draft #503 adds Pi screen sleep; CI and a real-Pi check remain.

## Oct 11

The boot screen now draws the tree on, and I designed a printable desk case for a 7-inch screen and a Pi. Curbfind went blue, and best deals is the default sort everywhere. Three AI models score each listing and drop junk ads. I built xreader, a bot that digests twenty AI accounts on X, and it runs only when I ask. Nimble's point update is in App Review. Plank can call AI models, run tools and give agents memory. Most of my web apps moved off Supabase onto one Cloudflare backend I built, spark-api. Passwords didn't come across, so everyone resets once. The installed iPhone and Mac apps still run on Supabase, and the garage site still forwards its leads there. Heyitsmejosh.com expires on the 13th and I'm not renewing it. The brand moves to jaybulb.com, which I'll buy in the morning.

Turing got three features in one night: she can report Joshua Tree's version, open PRs and whether qemu is running, the Mac app saves chats and brings them back, and a new message or "shh" stops her mid-sentence. The model itself barely got smarter. On a fresh 360-sentence test she scored 310. I trained a targeted run to push past that. It did worse on the standard set, so it stays on the shelf and the next run is up.

I started marketing my dad's garage door business in Langley with 37 Instagram posts, a carousel, a 30-day calendar and SEO fixes. The 17-second fake trailer, "Attack of the Garage Door", cost about $4 and went to him tonight, with the dance clip on the second try. The real Katana headlights shot is stuck until I pay for a Higgsfield app plan. I swapped in a cheap stand-in without asking, and I should have asked first. A stock voice sounded wrong, so he sends his own tomorrow.

I also emptied YouTube: 93 Watch Later videos sorted and summarized. Three retired projects got deleted too, all pushed first.
