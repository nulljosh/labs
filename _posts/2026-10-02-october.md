## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree reached 2.0 with twenty-six apps in protected boxes. Bookrank is live on iPhone with natural voices and offline reading.

Premium is $1 and auto-trading is live. My paper-traded Double 7s rule hit 77 percent. Notate is live on iPhone.

## Oct 4

A Pi 4B is coming for hardware tests.

My idea app became Hikko, since Hotaru was taken; iPhone and Mac are in review.

Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign and a privacy fix; iOS is live.

Turing shipped with file-picking answers, a new Mac icon and her own 1.5B model. Wrong picks fell under 10 per set; refusals fell to 2.6 percent.

## Oct 5

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a refreshed logo on Samantha's face.

Apple rejected seven apps as spam (Hamurapi, Windgate, Madobe, Mailbag, Curbfind, Plaintxt, Sidewise) and Hikko over screenshots.

Epiphany's map search uses Places now, and its iPhone build is in.

NYC Survive's overnight loop made it winnable; iOS is live, macOS in review.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a cache flush fix.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi after I wrote the login handshake its chip lacks, and the green light blinks again.

Samantha answers on the real Pi on my API credit. My Mac relay picks a cheap or strong model per question and gives her read-only access to one folder. An overnight runner works branches only, $5 cap per run. Money comes from a Pi dev kit; the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote Joshua Tree's eleven-chapter book with board photos.

The Pi got a calculator and a tiny language model. After a DNS fix it loaded heyitsmejosh.com over HTTPS; scripts still don't run. Every Pi image carried my relay key; the fix is in review, two more queued.

## Oct 9 to 10

I halved my custom skills and flashed the Pi card.

## Oct 11

I built xread, a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models. Most web apps moved from Supabase to spark-api, my Cloudflare backend, so everyone resets passwords. Heyitsmejosh.com lapses on the 13th; the brand moves to jaybulb.com. When spark-api stopped recognising sign-ins, apps fell back to the robot voice; the fix was approved today.

Turing can boot, type into and screenshot Joshua Tree from chat, on her own build machines only. I fixed 24 of 109 older abilities, and her new tool picker beat four of five test sets with fewer leaks.

I cleared 93 Watch Later videos and filed 69 X bookmarks into notes.

Four repos took new names: Healstack is Intake, Voxprint is Notate, Sparkjar is Hikko, Lexly is Tonchi. Eleven App Store listings moved to jaybulb.com. Nimble answers now carry photos and sources.

Twenty Joshua Tree changes merged, and my first Pi 4 run boots to a desktop. Bluetooth finds my mouse but hangs up before pairing. The 3.0 release stays a draft.

## Oct 12

Turing's picker clears all three dev bars, and its C tool fires on only 20 of 434 no-tool questions. The numbers are tuned to dev, so the sealed sets stay unread.

I folded ten Pi pull requests into one and flashed the card. It boots, and the mouse reconnects by itself. Multi-window is broken, the boot bar is choppy and the wallpaper is terracotta. Nimbus hit an A on round seven.

## Oct 13

I cut Joshua Tree's docs from fifty-six files to eleven. I approved a plan to make the OS a daily driver. On the real card my mouse survives reboot, but dragging redraws the whole screen. Turing's picker made 115 false calls against a bar of 22; an abstain step fixes that, PR open. Without a USB mouse, I re-paired Bluetooth and wrote a script.

I fixed my terminal shortcut and Codex's Stripe hooks; the restart is untested.

Evidna checks every one-letter change in a blood-protein gene.

## Oct 14

I built Noema on jaybulb.com with hands-on examples. First-descent checks passed past a million; algebra covers twenty-eight of thirty-two groups, and Lean checked one small proof. 

My YouTube summary skill moved into Codex, and forty of eighty math lectures are saved in iCloud.

## Oct 15

I synced sixty skills into Codex and merged fifty-seven Apple Notes into twenty-three. Four broken Stripe hooks are off.

## Oct 16

I filed 33 of 34 Apple Notes into roadmaps, then lost a day to updates.

I finished The Contrarian as one summary; How AI Thinks waits on my weekly limit.

jaybulb.com had no mail records, so nothing sent there could arrive. Now every address reaches my inbox.

Reset emails now carry each app's name. Quotestreak got a forgot-password page, and Brick's link has a new domain.

Hikko's sign-in hung on my dead domain; I moved it to jaybulb.com and fixed its emails to say Hikko.

Talli's landing, login and privacy pages went from cream to white. Its Stripe webhook had been rejecting purchases since Oct 2, fixed with a new signing secret. A locked BC Self-Serve account was behind its Invalid credentials error, not the code. Talli is down to three tabs and one big number on Home. I also fixed a Status tab that showed the wrong income, ran a security pass, and dropped payday push for now.

My portfolio moved to jaybulb.com; App Store apps keep the old address until next build.

Wordroot's homepage is now the app, styled like an Apple app. I dropped its custom font because it looked like Pokemon. Its iPhone and Mac builds wait on Apple, and the Watch app's photos ship with the next iPhone update.

## Oct 17

Tonchi is Patois, Hikko is Thinkpond and Windgate is Airwell, with repos and store listings renamed to match. All 41 apps got a brand kit and a new icon.

A security header had blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I patched about ten repos, and favicons went out on about thirty sites. Patois now teaches before it quizzes, live for Spanish units 1 to 5. Apple's rejections came from old builds calling my lapsed domain, so I swapped the hosts. Vancouver Vice's native shells went red when I moved only Apple's host, so all now use jaybulb.com. Thinkpond is back in App Review with a new build and demo account, and Mailbag, Epiphany and Intake need new builds to ship.

The limit on Unreal is RAM, not skill. I cleared 22 GB on my 16 GB Mac mini by moving old Claude chats and VM images to the LaCie. Booting the editor off that drive took 16 minutes, then a memory warning with swap nearly full, and I closed it. So Unreal gets one day a week while other projects are paused. The plan is a 1 TB SSD for the engine and selling the LaCie back to Dad, though renting a GPU by the hour is worth pricing first. Until then, optimization and the promo video happen on the web version.
