## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree reached 2.0 with twenty-six apps in protected boxes. Bookrank is live on iPhone with natural voices and offline reading. 

Premium is $1 and auto-trading is live. My paper-traded Double 7s stock rule hit 77 percent. Notate is live on iPhone.

## Oct 4

A Pi 4B is coming for hardware tests.

My idea app became Hikko, since Hotaru was taken; iPhone and Mac are in review.

Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign and a privacy fix; iOS is live, Mac in review.

Turing shipped with file-picking answers, a new Mac icon and her own 1.5B model. Wrong picks fell under 10 per set; refusals fell to 2.6 percent.

## Oct 5

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a refreshed logo on Samantha's face.

Apple rejected seven apps under spam: Hamurapi, Windgate, Madobe, Mailbag, Curbfind, Plaintxt, Sidewise; Hikko got stale screenshots. All five resubmitted.

Epiphany's map search uses Places now; iPhone submitted. Apple rejected Curbfind's update and I replied.

NYC Survive's overnight loop made it winnable; iOS is live, macOS in review. A headless bot wins it in two hours.

Nimble shipped spoken math.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a cache flush fix.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi; the chip has no login helper, so I wrote its WPA2 handshake and the green light blinks again.

Samantha answers on the real Pi using my API credit. My Mac relay picks a cheap or strong model per question and gives her read-only access to a shared folder.

An overnight runner works branches only, $5 cap per run.

Money comes from a Pi dev kit with a waitlist; the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote Joshua Tree’s eleven-chapter book with board photos.

The Pi got a scientific calculator and a tiny language model. After a DNS fix it loaded heyitsmejosh.com over HTTPS, its first web page; scripts still don't run.

Every Pi image carried my relay key. The fix is in review, with two more security fixes queued.

## Oct 9 to 10

I halved my custom skills and flashed the Pi card. Relay encryption and clock checks still need hardware.

## Oct 11

I built xread, a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models. Most web apps moved from Supabase to spark-api, my Cloudflare backend; passwords stayed behind, so everyone resets. Heyitsmejosh.com lapses on the 13th by choice; the brand moves to jaybulb.com.

The Mac app’s hideous layout became three columns. When spark-api stopped recognising installed apps' sign-in, they fell back to the robot voice; the fix was approved today.
Turing can boot, type into, screenshot and stop Joshua Tree from chat, and read its log. A boot found she could kill another session's build machine; now she stops only her own. I tested 109 of her older abilities and fixed 24. Her newest tool picker improved four of five test sets with fewer leaks; iPhone runs her tools too.

Dad's garage door marketing has 37 posts.

I cleared 93 Watch Later videos and filed 69 X bookmarks into notes.

Four repos took new names: Healstack is Intake, Voxprint is Notate, Sparkjar is Hikko, Lexly is Tonchi. Hacker News turned down Notate's Show HN, so I posted fourteen X launch threads. Eleven App Store listings moved to jaybulb.com. A Talli login bug on the new domain was a server allowlist I forgot.

Nimble answers carry photos, news has thumbnails, and answers cite up to three sources. I gave it a typeface, Nimble Sans, and a brand kit. It's with Apple.

Twenty Joshua Tree changes merged; my first Raspberry Pi 4 run boots to a desktop, search works, the beep plays and Calendar opens. Bluetooth finds my mouse, but hangs up before pairing. Tree Sans is live on the landing page; the OS-wide font is built, not pushed. The 3.0 release stays a draft until the board pass.

## Oct 12

Nimble shows photos matched to the answer's subject, credited to Wikipedia. Looping my exam burned the free AI quota, so the app shows a quiet resting note.

Turing’s picker chooses abilities per question and clears all three dev bars. The copy Joshua Tree carries is small C that stays out of plain chat, firing on 20 of 434 questions that needed no tool. The numbers are tuned to dev; the sealed sets stay unread.

The Pi stack folded ten pull requests into "the Pi gets every app," and I flashed and tested the card. It boots, and the Bluetooth mouse reconnects alone. Multi-window is still broken, the boot bar is choppy, and the wallpaper is terracotta. Nimbus hit an A on round seven.

## Oct 13

I cut Joshua Tree’s docs from fifty-six files to eleven; GitHub now says "A computer." The Pi release waits on test failures an agent is fixing. I approved a plan from many windows at 4.0 to a daily driver at 10.0. On the real 3.9 card, my mouse survives reboot, but dragging redraws the whole screen, causing lag, and only two windows open. Nimbus is on round eight with a print pack for a shop. Nimble got a three-mode privacy page, spoken conversations and Copy on Mac. Turing's picker made 115 false calls against a bar of 22; an abstain step now clears all three dev bars, with the tuning caveat and PR still open. Her iPhone voice answers aloud. Without a USB mouse, I re-paired Bluetooth and wrote a Connect script.

I fixed my terminal shortcut and Codex’s Stripe hooks, which lacked scripts. Seven passed; restarting Codex is untested, and updates could undo it.

Evidna on jaybulb.com checks every one-letter change in a blood-protein gene. Repeatable benchmarks separate calculations from experiments.

## Oct 14

I built Noema on jaybulb.com: blue, with hands-on math examples. First-descent checks passed past a million; algebra covers twenty-eight of thirty-two groups, and Lean checked one small proof. Collatz remains unsolved.

My YouTube summary skill moved into Codex; forty of eighty discrete math lectures are saved in iCloud. The rest waits on my allowance.

## Oct 15

Brain’s Codex connection follows the new domain. Unreal stays off while closed; Stripe needs my login.

I synced sixty skills into Codex and merged fifty-seven Apple Notes into twenty-three, keeping screenshots and links; the skill can repeat it. Four broken Stripe hooks are disabled and config checked; the warning is untested.

I emptied Watch Later, saved twelve iCloud notes and deleted the temp playlists.

## Oct 16

I filed 33 of 34 Apple Notes into repo roadmaps. Then a machine update day: package managers, Claude, and one Python helper I had to reinstall.

I finished the last two chapters of The Contrarian, merged the book into one summary and cleared the raw photos; How AI Thinks waits on my weekly limit.

jaybulb.com never had mail records, so nothing sent to it could arrive. I turned on Cloudflare's forwarding, so every address there lands in my inbox, and checked eleven of its hosts; ten answered fine.
