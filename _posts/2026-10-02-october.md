## Oct 1 to 2

Joshua Tree reached its 2.0 release. Twenty-six apps now run in protected boxes, and I fixed the last flaky tests. Bookrank is live on iPhone with natural voices, Goodreads import, offline reading and highlights. Plank shipped a big update, and Conveyer launched a second rocket. All my apps are now 99 cents.

Epiphany got much faster. A map that took 23 seconds to load now takes under 4. Its Premium plan is $1, and its auto-trading feature is live. I also tested a stock rule called Double 7s on paper money: it buys after ten-day lows and sells after highs, and it won about 77 percent of the time on past data.

Talli was rejected because my test account did not work, so I fixed it and resubmitted. Healstack needed Sign in with Apple and was resubmitted. Notate went live on iPhone at 99 cents.

## Oct 3

Joshua Tree shipped six versions. A 30-second intro with cloned voice, face with matching lip movement, Music and Movies apps. Tests now run in 10 minutes. Turing answers as me as well as Samantha.

I started moving Joshua Tree to Raspberry Pi hardware. Screen drawing works; everything else waits on the real device.

Fixes went out across several apps too. Windgate now shows minutes in plain words. Lexly was resubmitted with its new Pre-Calc 12 course. Notate's next version is ready. Every app got a legal setting that skips a form on first build.

## Oct 4

A Raspberry Pi 4B is on the way to test Joshua Tree on real hardware. Apps on the Pi run in locked rooms, and Bookrank, Tonchi and Hikko show real data. Fixed Burrow cutting names, Stocks and Mail losing files, menu bar staying after Launchpad (PR 406 merged). PR 405 centered the Launchpad grid with smaller icons; it's in CI. Disk got full so I cleaned it. The roadmap now queues every fleet app for Launchpad (18 apps). Two helpers building: Windgate and Samantha video mode (fixes due tomorrow).

Madobe's landing loads any site through a Worker proxy, stripping cookies.

My idea-catching app became Hikko (Hotaru was taken). New look: near-white paper with terracotta accent. Fixed the daily generator repeating variants. iPhone and Mac 3.0 are in review; Mac got terracotta in buttons and sidebar. Blockframe, my wireframe tool, now says Blockframe everywhere. New build heading to Apple.

Mailbag (renamed from Hagaki) is my mail-filing app. Fixed Mac window focus and demo sign-in corruption; sorter tightened. Cleared iCloud from 21 to 0, Gmail through app sign-in. Two-reviewer review found wrong filed/archived count; fixed. Build bumped locally. Store not submitted (listing still Hagaki, 1.0 in review). Blocked: Gmail sign-in did not persist on Mac, Outlook parked.

Lexly became Tonchi (quick wit in Japanese). Content grew from 45 to 165 courses; lessons open with a learn-first card. Web moved to tonchi.heyitsmejosh.com, GitHub to nulljosh/tonchi. Tested 32 language versions, kept 14. Programming tab split three ways. GitHub issues from 80 to 2.

Healstack got a major web redesign: three-tab layout like Apple Health, white theme, blue accent. Fixed a privacy bug where new accounts inherited a hardcoded profile; now empty. Renaming it Intake: folder and repo moved today. iOS approved and live; Mac in review.

Turing shipped with file-picking answers and a new Mac icon. The picker is now her own 1.5B model on Mac, published on Hugging Face (850 MB, downloads in background; Windows and Linux stay smaller). Wrong picks are under 10 per set; missed commands 5 percent against a 2 percent goal, so the major shipped documented with the next round queued. Landing and stats live.

v5.1 is in progress: guard fixes cut refusals to 2.6 percent, but 10 picks still leak. Needs per-tool adversarial review. v6.0 target is tomorrow: Samantha on Joshua Tree and Pi.

## Oct 5

Turing shipped with guard letting confident non-writing picks run without cue words. Built app/robot.py (Linux voice), tested on Mac. Round 16 hit diminishing returns; loop paused. docs/PI.md updated, tests/test_robot.py added.

Joshua Tree landing page opens straight on Samantha's face. New logo with rounded forks and engraved grooves waiting on GitHub. OS reached 29 apps; compression cut program size by half. Drove into Vancouver looking for a Raspberry Pi.

Hamurapi (1968 king game with drawn city, web, Mac, iPhone, terminal, and Joshua Tree port) is waiting for App Store review on both iOS and macOS. Free one week then ninety-nine cents. Joshua called it the most ambitious ask: OS built with Claude, game running in the OS.

Brick (real estate browser) went live with listings from HousingFeed and RentCast. AI mode filters homes by plain words; deal score ranks by percent under typical. iPhone fills the screen with a map. Landing has a 27-second video with feature cards. Desktop and Android are building in CI, tests green. App Store listing pending from a sign-in 503; retry scheduled.

Bookrank shipped an update on iPhone. The app now fills missing book covers by looking them up on Open Library and saves them to the database, mirroring the web. I added three covers to books.json; seven still need images. The build went to App Review.

Epiphany's map search fixed: Places fills from Apple Maps first (under a second), swaps to fuller list when Overpass answers. Search bar got Done button and clear button; What's New moved to card. Fixed machine-wide SwiftLint plugin issue breaking command-line builds. iPhone submitted. QA found money missing separators and blank Autopilot card; PR 189 open but untested yet.

Conveyer retired the beaten world; skill picker played live. Claude passed iron_plate_throughput by hand. Game Dev Tycoon is the next game. Three new games from Steam (FEZ, Papers Please, Game Dev Tycoon).

Bonsai 2 passed all 14 test tasks with thinking on. Built a Python agent with offline tool calls.

Hikko iOS 3.0 was rejected (reading why blocked on Apple login); everything else live or in review. Tonchi (ex-Lexly) resubmitted with new Pre-Calc 12 course. Notate on iPhone at 99 cents. Talli resubmitted with fixed test account. Healstack (now Intake) resubmitted with Sign in with Apple. Curvely and Numen are next in the queue.

Cruise (the Duolingo autoplayer) nearly lost its 180-day streak when the runner crashed and never restarted. Added a 60-second watchdog in the status app to bring it back. Streak guard plays a language after 6pm with zero XP. Measured XP per hour: Polish 1,300, chess 520. Switched to Polish as default; fastest-track picker chooses the best earner. Cleaned disk from 2.2GB to 6.9GB. Goal: 500k XP by tomorrow.

Tonchi grew dramatically: bigger Tatoeba courses (CC-BY licensed) for all 11 languages Cruise played, ordered by the words Cruise actually practised. Greek and Polish each have 120 units, Hindi 55, Japanese 49, Korean 43, French 36. Added a new chess exercise type: 195 Lichess puzzles (CC0) on a real FEN board for iOS and macOS. But the Duolingo-captured drills that got imported earlier contradict the ATTRIBUTION.md claim of only open sources; those units need attribution work before going back in the catalog.
