## Oct 1 to 2

Joshua Tree reached 2.0, with twenty-six apps in protected boxes and flaky tests fixed. Bookrank is live on iPhone with natural voices, Goodreads import, offline reading and highlights. All my apps are now 99 cents.

Epiphany maps load in under 4 seconds, down from 23. Premium plan is $1 and auto-trading is live. A stock rule I paper-traded, Double 7s, hit 77 percent.
Talli was rejected over a broken test account and resubmitted. Notate went live on iPhone at 99 cents.

## Oct 3

Joshua Tree shipped six versions with a 30-second intro and Music and Movies apps. I started moving the kernel to a Pi.
## Oct 4

A Pi 4B is on the way to test Joshua Tree on real hardware. Apps on the Pi run in locked rooms, and Bookrank, Tonchi and Hikko show real data.

My idea-catching app became Hikko (Hotaru was taken), with near-white paper and a terracotta accent. iPhone and Mac 3.0 are in review; Mac got terracotta buttons and sidebar.

Mailbag is my mail app. Fixed Mac window focus, demo sign-in and the sorter. Blocked: Gmail sign-in on Mac.

Lexly became Tonchi. Content grew to 165 courses.

Healstack got a web redesign and a privacy fix. iOS live; Mac in review.

Turing shipped with file-picking answers and new Mac icon, her own 1.5B model (850 MB). Wrong picks under 10 per set. v5.1 cut refusals to 2.6 percent.

## Oct 5

Joshua Tree landing is live with a refreshed logo on Samantha's face.

Apple rejected seven apps under spam: Hamurapi, Windgate, Madobe, Mailbag, Curbfind, Plaintxt, Sidewise; Hikko got stale-screenshots. Plaintxt now has line numbers, Go to Line and preview. Sidewise rebuilt with Compare, Share extension, widget. Madobe iOS and macOS got Mac Float, Pair pages, per-site rules, App Intents. All five resubmitted.
Epiphany's map search now uses Places; iPhone submitted. Curbfind shipped Near me and a smarter ranker; Apple rejected it and I replied. Conveyer retired the beaten world.

NYC Survive's overnight loop made it winnable and shipped it (iOS live, macOS in review). Recruits tie to beds and food, squads take orders, and night raids come unless you set PATROL. A headless bot wins it in about two hours.

## Oct 5 to 6

Swept 15 Apple Notes into project roadmaps and emptied Notes.app. Nimble shipped spoken math and dropped "Answers" from its store name.

Notate, Talli and Plank got UI fixes, and Joshua Tree added a desktop button on the hero.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a CPU cache flush fix. The USB driver worked in QEMU, then on the Pi with a cable swap. A Claude relay lets my Mac build the OS inside it.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi. The chip has no login helper, so I wrote the WPA2 handshake, and the green light blinks again.

Samantha answers on the real Pi through my API credit. A relay on my Mac picks a cheap or strong model per question. She can read a shared Mac folder, read-only. 

An agent wrote the Pi-side actions on its branch, so asking Samantha now prints a note and blinks the light. The safety check rightly blocked a Codex mode that let anyone on the network run programs on my Mac, so Codex waits for a secure tunnel. An overnight runner works branches only, $5 cap per run.

Next: split the kernel's giant C file, then HTTPS on the Pi. Money comes from a Pi dev kit with a waitlist live, and the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote the Joshua Tree story as an eleven-chapter book with board photos. The Pi's first benchmark: memory copies about 1100 MB/s, Wi-Fi in 6.4 seconds, clock in 8.7.

The Pi got a scientific calculator and a tiny language model. The browser stalled on DNS, and after the fix the Pi loaded heyitsmejosh.com over HTTPS, the first real web page on the board, though it still cannot run scripts.

I also found a real hole: every Pi image carried my relay key, not just the dev card. The fix is in review, and two more are on the list: the key goes over Wi-Fi in plain text, and the clock trusts an unchecked date header.

## Oct 9

I halved my custom skills, fixed Pi keyboard paths and flashed the checked card. Signup email arrived; I redesigned it, fixed mobile signup, tightened Samantha's shared-folder access, and wrote the Wi-Fi guide. Relay encryption and clock protection still need hardware checks.

## Oct 10

The landing refresh is live, QA passed. Draft #503 adds Pi screen sleep; CI and a real-Pi check remain.

## Oct 11

I built xread, a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can now call AI models. Most of my web apps moved off Supabase onto spark-api, a Cloudflare backend I built. Passwords didn't move, so everyone resets once. Heyitsmejosh.com lapses on the 13th by my choice, so the brand moves to jaybulb.com. I built its landing page with a yellow sidebar and a bulb-and-pencil logo, and pointed every launch kit at it.

My Goodreads shelves now sit under my summaries, with half stars and a mini player. I tested the Mac app myself, called the first layout hideous, and made it a Mail-style three-column window. The installed apps had quietly dropped to the robot voice, since spark-api stopped recognising their Supabase sign-in. The last version got approved today. The Mac account screen was clipped, so it scrolls. The voice meter had been stuck on Checking, and every native user shared one daily cap, so each person has their own now.

Turing had a long night of releases. From chat she can boot, type into, screenshot and stop Joshua Tree, and read its serial log. I tried 109 of her older abilities for real and fixed 24. A real boot found two bugs, one that could have killed another session's build machine, so she now only stops the machine she started. The picker sharpened: 802 right tool picks out of 869 sealed tests. The image model test waits for 10 GB of free memory.

I started marketing my dad's Langley garage door business with 37 Instagram posts and a 30-day calendar. The 17-second fake trailer cost $4. The real Katana headlights shot needs a paid Higgsfield app plan, so I swapped in a cheap stand-in without asking. A stock voice sounded wrong, so he records his own.

I cleared 93 YouTube Watch Later videos, deleted three retired projects, and pulled my 69 saved X bookmarks into my notes, read the five long articles among them, and unbookmarked the lot. X blocked a login from my automation, so xread now drives a plain Chrome with its own profile over a debug port, and the scripts live in dotfiles.

Healstack is Intake, Voxprint is Notate, Sparkjar is Hikko and Lexly is Tonchi, in the repos and launch material. Hacker News turned down Notate's Show HN because my account is too new, so I posted fourteen X launch threads instead.
