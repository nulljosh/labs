## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree reached 2.0 with twenty-six apps in protected boxes. Bookrank is live on iPhone with natural voices and offline reading.

Premium is $1 and auto-trading is live. My paper-traded Double 7s rule hit 77 percent. Notate is live on iPhone.

## Oct 4

A Pi 4B is coming for hardware tests.

My idea app became Hikko, since Hotaru was taken; iPhone and Mac are in review.

Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign; iOS is live.


## Oct 5

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a refreshed logo on Samantha's face.

Apple rejected seven apps as spam and Hikko over screenshots.


NYC Survive's overnight loop made it winnable; iOS is live, macOS in review.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a cache flush fix.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi after I wrote the login handshake its chip lacked.

Samantha answers on the real Pi on my API credit. My Mac relay picks a cheap or strong model per question and gives her read-only access to one folder. An overnight runner works branches only, $5 cap per run. Money comes from a Pi dev kit; the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote Joshua Tree's eleven-chapter book with board photos.

After a DNS fix it loaded heyitsmejosh.com over HTTPS; scripts still don't run. Every Pi image carried my relay key; the fix is in review.

## Oct 9 to 10

I halved my custom skills and flashed the Pi card.

## Oct 11

I built xread, a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models. Most web apps moved off Supabase, so everyone resets passwords. Heyitsmejosh.com lapses on the 13th; the brand moves to jaybulb.com.

Turing can drive Joshua Tree from chat on her build machines only. I fixed 24 of 109 older abilities, and her new tool picker beat four of five test sets with fewer leaks.


Four repos took new names and eleven App Store listings moved to jaybulb.com. 
Twenty Joshua Tree changes merged, and my first Pi 4 run boots to a desktop. Bluetooth finds my mouse but hangs up before pairing.

## Oct 12

Turing's picker clears all three dev bars, and its C tool fires on only 20 of 434 no-tool questions. The numbers are tuned to dev, so the sealed sets stay unread.

I folded ten Pi pull requests into one and flashed the card. It boots, and the mouse reconnects by itself. Multi-window is broken, the boot bar is choppy and the wallpaper is terracotta.

## Oct 13

I cut Joshua Tree's docs from fifty-six files to eleven. I approved a plan to make the OS a daily driver. On the real card my mouse survives reboot, but dragging redraws the whole screen. Turing's picker made 115 false calls against a bar of 22; an abstain step fixes that, PR open.


## Oct 14

I built Noema on jaybulb.com. First-descent checks passed past a million, and Lean checked one small proof.


## Oct 15

I synced sixty skills into Codex and merged fifty-seven Apple Notes into twenty-three. Four broken Stripe hooks are off.

## Oct 16

I filed 33 of 34 Apple Notes into roadmaps, then lost a day to updates.

jaybulb.com had no mail records, so nothing sent there could arrive. Now every address reaches my inbox.

Reset emails now carry each app's name. Quotestreak got a forgot-password page, and Brick's link has a new domain.

Hikko's sign-in hung on my dead domain, so I moved it.

Talli's pages went from cream to white. Its Stripe webhook had rejected purchases since Oct 2, fixed with a new signing secret. A locked BC Self-Serve account, not the code, caused its Invalid credentials error.

My portfolio moved to jaybulb.com; App Store apps keep the old address until next build.

Wordroot's homepage is now the app, in Apple style, and I dropped its custom font because it looked like Pokemon. Its iPhone and Mac builds wait on Apple.

## Oct 17

Tonchi is Patois, Hikko is Thinkpond and Windgate is Airwell, with repos and listings renamed to match. All 41 apps got a brand kit and a new icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I patched about ten repos and sent favicons to about thirty sites. Patois now teaches before it quizzes, live for Spanish units 1 to 5. Apple's rejections came from old builds calling my lapsed domain, so I swapped the hosts. Thinkpond is back in App Review with a new build and demo account, and Mailbag, Epiphany and Intake need new builds to ship.

The limit on Unreal is RAM, not skill. Clearing old chats and VM images freed 22 GB on my 16 GB Mac mini, but the editor still took 16 minutes to boot from the LaCie and then ran out of memory. Unreal gets one day a week until a 1 TB SSD lands and the LaCie goes back to Dad; renting a GPU by the hour is worth pricing first. Until then the work stays on the web.

## Oct 18

Cruise had slowed from about 16k XP a day to 11k, and I found why. Balance mode kept picking the course furthest behind, so the runner spent 11 hours on Japanese and 8 on chess, both slow earners, while Turkish, Hindi, Greek and Polish make 1,200 to 1,580 XP an hour. Now a course must be fast enough to get picked. Cruise is at 602,884 XP with a 193-day streak. A Turkish match bug stalled that course for a day; the fix is in but unproven live.

Atlin got a folded-map-and-pin icon and blue sign-up emails. Splash, onboarding, Settings and a save prompt all shipped and passed QA. Both the iPhone and Mac builds are back in Apple's queue.

Turing can now render a Blender file, fetch web files into Downloads and read Hacker News for research, and she moved to turing.jaybulb.com. I audited the GitHub issues, reopened 29 closed without proof, and closed only the ones I could check. Four models built a walkable 3D copy of my bedroom from nine photos; Opus looked most like the room.

Joshua Tree has been red for a while. Every Chat check failed on every pull request and on main, because heyitsmejosh.com lapsed and the kernel fetched Samantha's face from that old host. Her portrait never loaded, so her captions never drew. The domain move and a check that waits for her caption to ease in are both in CI now. Nothing merged this stretch. The windows rework is done on my machine: up to four apps open at once, and one can crash while the others keep running. It passes the gate and is up for review. Next in line: a better close button on Samantha's full screen, since the red dot looks like a record light, a simpler docs and architecture diagram, and then "Talk to it." One heavy job at a time.
