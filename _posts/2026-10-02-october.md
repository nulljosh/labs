## Oct 1 to 2

Joshua Tree reached its 2.0 release. Twenty-six apps now run in protected boxes, and I fixed the last flaky tests. Bookrank is live on iPhone with natural voices, Goodreads import, offline reading and highlights. Plank shipped a big update, and Conveyer launched a second rocket. All my apps are now 99 cents.

## Oct 6

The Raspberry Pi 4 arrived and Joshua Tree booted on real hardware for the first time. The picture was half size and the console text came out as one-pixel marks, because the CPU cache wasn't flushing to RAM. By evening it was fixed: full 1920x1080, readable text, and a line above the dock for Steve Jobs that ends with the title of the Steve Jobs Archive book I'm reading. I wrote the USB driver in QEMU first, and a NuPhy keyboard typed on the Pi the moment I swapped a charge-only cable for a real data cable. The Wi-Fi code is on the card and boots, but its lines scroll off the screen, so I'm adding Page Up scrolling to see whether it works. A Claude app for Joshua Tree now talks through a relay on my Mac, and the goal is Claude Code running inside my own OS on the Pi, helping build it. The roadmap is pruned to a top ten, backed by five rounds of research on what Linux, macOS and others have that we don't. I wrote a shopping list and seven standing decisions, and cleaned the repo down to four open pull requests and no issues.
