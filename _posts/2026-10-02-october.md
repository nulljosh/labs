## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree reached 2.0 with twenty-six apps in protected boxes. Bookrank is live on iPhone with natural voices and offline reading. 

Premium is $1 and auto-trading is live. My paper-traded Double 7s rule hit 77 percent. Notate is live on iPhone.

## Oct 4

A Pi 4B is coming for hardware tests.

My idea app became Hikko, since Hotaru was taken; iPhone and Mac are in review.

Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign and a privacy fix; iOS is live, Mac in review.

Turing shipped with file-picking answers, a new Mac icon and her own 1.5B model. Wrong picks fell under 10 per set; refusals fell to 2.6 percent.

## Oct 5

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a refreshed logo on Samantha's face.

Apple rejected seven apps under spam: Hamurapi, Windgate, Madobe, Mailbag, Curbfind, Plaintxt, Sidewise; Hikko got stale screenshots. All five resubmitted.

Epiphany's map search uses Places now; iPhone submitted. Apple rejected Curbfind's update.

NYC Survive's overnight loop made it winnable, and a headless bot wins it in two hours; iOS is live, macOS in review.

Nimble shipped spoken math.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a cache flush fix.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi after I wrote the login handshake its chip lacks, and the green light blinks again.

Samantha answers on the real Pi on my API credit. My Mac relay picks a cheap or strong model per question and gives her read-only access to one folder.

An overnight runner works branches only, $5 cap per run.

Money comes from a Pi dev kit with a waitlist; the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote Joshua Tree’s eleven-chapter book with board photos.

The Pi got a calculator and a tiny language model. After a DNS fix it loaded heyitsmejosh.com over HTTPS; scripts still don't run.

Every Pi image carried my relay key; the fix is in review, two more queued.

## Oct 9 to 10

I halved my custom skills and flashed the Pi card. Relay encryption and clock checks need hardware.

## Oct 11

I built xread, a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models. Most web apps moved from Supabase to spark-api, my Cloudflare backend, so everyone resets passwords. Heyitsmejosh.com lapses on the 13th; the brand moves to jaybulb.com.

The Mac app’s layout became three columns. When spark-api stopped recognising sign-ins, apps fell back to the robot voice; the fix was approved today.
Turing can boot, type into, screenshot and stop Joshua Tree from chat, and read its log, but only her own build machines now. I tested 109 older abilities and fixed 24. Her new tool picker beat four of five test sets with fewer leaks; iPhone runs her tools too.

I cleared 93 Watch Later videos and filed 69 X bookmarks into notes.

Four repos took new names: Healstack is Intake, Voxprint is Notate, Sparkjar is Hikko, Lexly is Tonchi. Eleven App Store listings moved to jaybulb.com.

Nimble answers carry photos, news has thumbnails and answers cite sources. I gave it a typeface and a brand kit; it's with Apple.

Twenty Joshua Tree changes merged; my first Pi 4 run boots to a desktop, search works, the beep plays. Bluetooth finds my mouse but hangs up before pairing. The 3.0 release stays a draft.

## Oct 12

Nimble shows photos matched to each answer, credited to Wikipedia. Looping my exam burned the free AI quota, so the app shows a quiet note.

Turing’s picker clears all three dev bars. Its C tool stays out of plain chat, firing on 20 of 434 no-tool questions. Numbers are tuned to dev; the sealed sets stay unread.

I folded ten Pi pull requests into one and flashed the card. It boots, and the mouse reconnects by itself. Multi-window is broken, the boot bar is choppy and the wallpaper is terracotta. Nimbus hit an A on round seven.

## Oct 13

I cut Joshua Tree’s docs from fifty-six files to eleven; GitHub now says "A computer." The Pi release waits on test fixes. I approved a plan to make the OS a daily driver. On the real card my mouse survives reboot, but dragging redraws the whole screen. Nimble got a privacy page and Copy on Mac. Turing's picker made 115 false calls against a bar of 22; an abstain step now clears all three dev bars, PR open. Without a USB mouse, I re-paired Bluetooth and wrote a script.

I fixed my terminal shortcut and Codex’s Stripe hooks; the restart is untested.

Evidna checks every one-letter change in a blood-protein gene. Repeatable benchmarks separate calculations from experiments.

## Oct 14

I built Noema on jaybulb.com with hands-on examples. First-descent checks passed past a million; algebra covers twenty-eight of thirty-two groups, and Lean checked one small proof. Collatz remains unsolved.

My YouTube summary skill moved into Codex; forty of eighty discrete math lectures are saved in iCloud; the rest waits on my allowance.

## Oct 15

Brain’s Codex connection follows the new domain. Unreal stays off while closed; Stripe needs my login.

I synced sixty skills into Codex and merged fifty-seven Apple Notes into twenty-three, keeping screenshots and links. Four broken Stripe hooks are off.

## Oct 16

I filed 33 of 34 Apple Notes into repo roadmaps, then lost a day to updates.

I finished The Contrarian as one summary; How AI Thinks waits on my weekly limit.

jaybulb.com had no mail records, so nothing sent there could arrive. Every address reaches my inbox, Primitive too.

Reset emails carry each app's name, checked in the Resend log for all ten web apps. Quotestreak got a forgot-password page, and Brick's link has a new domain. I reset my shared login and tested the link. Mail with no redirect is refused, so no generic Jaybulb mail goes out.

Hikko's sign-in hung on my dead domain; I moved it to jaybulb.com and fixed the emails to say Hikko.

Talli's landing, login and privacy pages went from cream to white, as did Keyrate's and Wordroot's, and both lost their demos. Plank went from beige to neutral paper, and Talli's Stripe webhook rejected purchases since Oct 2; a new signing secret fixed it.

I moved my portfolio to jaybulb.com; App Store apps keep the old address until the next build.

Weather got a real icon, a sun behind a cloud on blue. Wordroot is now the app on its homepage, with its own typeface and a blue branching-tree icon, and the new iPhone build is waiting on Apple with new screenshots.

I cut my Claude commands from 29 to 17, folding fourteen into /pitch and /person. The menu bar now shows wifi, and my Claude usage meter keeps its last good reading when the check gets rate-limited.
