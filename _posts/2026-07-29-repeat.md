Three weeks of mostly App Store paperwork. The big renames all landed: Books became Uprighty, Brief became Litigate, Grapher became Curvely, and Echo became Voxprint after Apple called the name too generic. Un-paywalled every Lexly course, since the payment gate had never actually worked.

Found and fixed a real security problem: Litigate's site was serving live court documents to anyone who asked, hidden behind a CSS overlay that protected nothing. Moved it behind a proper auth gate and took an old personal page offline that had started ranking on Google.

Two records requests came back on the 2021 case. No body-worn camera footage ever existed, and the 911 tape was destroyed under the retention policy. Filed both, follow-up queued.

## Sunday (August 2)

Finally killed the colonist autoplay bug in the Times Square game. I had assumed the directive engine was the whole cause, but two other systems were also assigning jobs on their own, so the colonists kept working no matter what the player did. Removed both and cleared out months of dead autopilot code.

## Monday (August 3)

Sorted out Epiphany's payments. The live key had been mangled during an old debugging session, so the webhook had been quietly failing ever since, and the stale monthly subscription became a one dollar unlock. Tried to roll payments out everywhere else and stopped, because most apps have no premium feature worth gating.

Ended the night killing a daily annoyance: every morning I re-authenticated the App Store session by hand, when the session was usually still alive and the password was already in my keychain. Now one command checks first and only asks when it actually expired.

## Apps

Uprighty live. Litigate's leak closed. Lexly and Sparkjar unblocked. Voxprint renamed and resubmitted. Quotable's poster art backfilled from a new source after the old one died.
