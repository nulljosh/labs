## Saturday

Caught three user-facing bugs nobody guessed going in: the notification panel's "wrong font" was the cursor antialiasing text underneath; the dock hover stuck-magnify was two stacked issues (starved page-table cap plus a hit-test off by half a tile since v15); the weather had been asking about the wrong city the whole time. Added a real syscall gate, ring-3 task teardown, VMware absolute-mouse, documentation to 100%, and a CI workflow that runs on every push; honest about scope: Epiphany needs TLS, plain-HTTP stock APIs don't exist, multi-window is four steps deliberately deferred.
