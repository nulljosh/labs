## Thursday (August 13)

Spent the day fixing production bugs across eleven repos that had never made it onto any list. Litigate's light mode had never worked, Sparkjar's account deletion was erroring out, and Talli's sync had been wedged for months while cheerfully reporting fresh timestamps. The pattern was the same every time: the roadmap said done, and it had never actually shipped.

## Friday (August 14)

Rebuilt Lexly for the Mac with a sign-in fix that was written the day before and never actually built. Also corrected a tidy story I had been telling myself, that the three sign-in rejections shared one root cause. They had three unrelated causes and only looked alike from the outside.

## Saturday (August 15)

Ran updates and they reported success while lying. Two package upgrades raced each other, and Go quietly lost its install directory and disappeared from my path. The breakage was not the annoying part. Nothing failed loudly enough for me to notice.

## Sunday (August 16)

Another sweep where half the work turned out to be already done and the roadmaps were simply wrong about it. Went looking anyway and found a real one: new Lexly accounts could be left stranded on the confirmation screen forever. Then discovered every API key on this machine is dead, because a rotation months ago updated the hosting provider and never touched the copies on disk.

## Monday (August 17)

Rewrote both of the apps Apple flagged for quality from web-view shells into real native ones, which meant writing my own expression parser to replace the maths library. Checked it by comparing several thousand results against the original, and they match.

Also finished the book backlog and have to record a loss: four chapters ended up as one paragraph stubs because the checks passed them before the source photos were deleted. The book went back to the library on the eleventh, so those are gone unless I borrow it again.

## Apps

Nine production sites fixed. Wiretext and Curvely now genuinely native. Everything else waiting on the freeze.
