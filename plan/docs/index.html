Education & Career Roadmap

CS Plan

School

SFU Computing Science — real Bachelor of Science, Burnaby, Lower Mainland, easier admission than UBC.

Ruled out: UVic (not Lower Mainland) · KPU (no real CS degree) · BCIT (legit diploma/degree, but too vocational — closes the engineering door)

Path

Degree, not diploma. Take calc + physics year 1 to keep engineering optional. Finish the CS degree with a Finance minor at SFU Beedie, or skip the minor and do a Master of Science in Finance (MSc Finance) at SFU after. Reassess after 2 years.

Finance Track — SFU

CS is the base. Finance is the multiplier — it's what turns a dev job into quant/fintech/trading pay. Two ways to add it, pick one at end of year 2:

OptionWhat it costsBest if
Finance minor
Beedie, ~7 courses inside the BSc		 No extra years, no extra tuition beyond normal course load. Needs BUS 217/312 + electives. You want to graduate on time and work as a dev at a fintech/bank.
MSc Finance
Beedie, ~12–16 months post-BSc		 ~$35–45k tuition, one extra year of no salary. Needs decent GPA + GMAT/GRE. You want quant / portfolio / trading roles, not just engineering.

Default: take the minor. It's free relative to the degree and keeps the master's open — MSc Finance admits CS grads, and a Finance minor + CS BSc is a stronger application than CS alone. Decide on the master's after you've worked 1–2 years and know whether you actually want the quant side.

Practical note: keep first-year calc and stats strong — both the minor and MSc lean on them, and it's the same calc you're already taking to keep engineering open. No extra prereq cost.

Cost

Moving to Vancouver (Kits or Chinatown)

No student debt (living at home + covered tuition) plus entry-level pay clears Kits/Chinatown rent comfortably from year one of working. On the savings model above, $500k lands around year 12, $1M around year 17 — both while renting in Vancouver, not after.
