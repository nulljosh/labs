# Terms

*Last updated October 2026*

By using Irisa you agree to these rules. They apply on the web and in the apps.

## Who can use it
Adults 18 and over. If you are younger, do not use Irisa.

## What is not allowed
- Nudity or anything sexual, on camera or in chat.
- Harassment, hate, threats, or slurs.
- Anyone under 18 on camera.
- Recording or sharing someone else's call without their consent.
- Spam, ads, bots, or impersonation.

## What we do about it
Report cuts the call. Three reports from different people in a day bans that person for a day, and we review reports by hand. Block means you are never matched with that person again. We can ban anyone who breaks these rules, at any time, without warning.

## No promises
Irisa is free and offered as it is. You meet strangers at your own risk. Never share your address, passwords, or money with someone you met here.
