Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

Irisa is video chat with strangers. It collects as little as it can.

What leaves your device

  • Camera and microphone. Used only during a call, only with your permission. Video and audio go straight to the other person and are never recorded or stored.
  • Text chat and your handle. Passed through our server to the person you are talking to, and never stored. Your handle only reaches them if you both tap Keep.
  • Reports and blocks. When you report or block someone, we keep a salted hash of both network addresses and the reason you gave. Reports are deleted after seven days. Blocks are kept so you are never matched again. We cannot turn the hash back into an address.
  • Site analytics. The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across sites.

What stays on your device

Your handle and that you confirmed you are 18 or older.

What we never do

No ads. No selling data. No tracking cookies. No accounts.

Age

Irisa is for adults 18 and over only.
