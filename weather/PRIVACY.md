# Weather Privacy Policy

*Last updated September 2026*

Weather is a forecast. It doesn't want your data, and it collects as little as it can.

## What leaves your device
- **Location.** Your location is used only to fetch the forecast, and only if you allow it.
- **Site analytics.** The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across sites.

## What we never do
No ads. No selling data. No tracking cookies.

## Kids
Weather isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
