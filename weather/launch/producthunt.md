First comment (maker story):
Weather does one job. You open it and get the answer: the temperature now, what it feels like, and the week ahead.

Plenty of weather apps show a lot more than you asked for. This one shows the forecast and nothing else. I wanted it to stay that small, so there's no account, no ads and no app to install. It's a web page, so there's nothing to download and nothing to sign into.

Search by name, or tap once for where you are. Seven days show highs, lows and conditions. Your Celsius or Fahrenheit pick is remembered, so the next visit doesn't ask again.

It follows your system theme, so it's dark or light to match the rest of your screen. There's no pile of settings to dig through.

It runs on Cloudflare Workers. It's one small web app, and that's the whole thing.
