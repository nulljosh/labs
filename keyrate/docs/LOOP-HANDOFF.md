# Keyrate loop handoff (2026-11-02, 23:15)

## What the loop is

A self-paced `/loop` that works the gap matrix first (G1 to G8), then `roadmap.md` one small step at a time. Each step ends with its check green, and each step gets a QA pass with screenshots sent to Joshua before the next one starts. Keyrate stays one number with nothing attached: no accounts, themes, multiplayer or leaderboards.

The loop never uses cron, launchd or a cloud routine. Nothing gets pushed until `node --test`, `./check.sh` and the headless web smoke test pass.

## Where things stand

- **Web:** live at keyrate.jaybulb.com. The test runs at /app and sits under the landing hero. The result has a WPM-over-time curve, and the mode bar is a glass layer. Rules live in `session.js`, the score math in `score.js`.
- **Shared scoring:** 30 score vectors in `vectors.json`. The web, Kotlin and Swift versions all have to agree on them.
- **iPhone, iPad and Mac:** SwiftUI app in `apple/`, generated by xcodegen. It has an animated launch splash, no title, an inset glass mode bar and fixed iPad orientations. The iPhone, iPad and Mac screenshots are uploaded.
- **Android and desktop:** Kotlin Multiplatform app in `kmp/`. Scoring is ported and tested. It has never run on a device.
- **App Store:** record 6830043245, exact name Keyrate, free, all 175 territories. Privacy label "Data Not Collected" is published. Version 2.0.0 build 4 for iOS and Mac is submitting. Joshua's steps are the Apple login and 2FA, Play sign-in, signing and notarizing.
- **Icon and brand:** a staggered keyboard (four keys, three keys, space bar). Light mode is white on web and native; dark is the shared warm dark. `brand/BRAND.md` matches what ships.
- **Gap matrix:** G1 (WPM curve) is done on the web. The native curve is still open.
