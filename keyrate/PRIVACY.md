# Keyrate Privacy Policy

*Last updated November 2026*

Keyrate is a typing test. It collects nothing.

## What stays on your device
Your best speed for each mode and your last 30 results (the website also remembers the mode you last picked). Nothing else is saved, and it never leaves the device:

- **Web:** your browser's local storage for keyrate.jaybulb.com. Clear the site's data and it's gone.
- **iPhone, iPad and Mac:** the app's own settings on that device.
- **Android:** the app's private storage, kept out of cloud backups and phone-to-phone transfers.
- **Mac, Windows and Linux desktop:** your user preferences on that computer, plus where you left the window.
- **Terminal:** a `.keyrate` file in your home folder.

## What leaves your device
Nothing you type and no score. None of the apps make a network call. The website is served by Cloudflare and loads its stylesheet from jaybulb.com; like any web host, they handle the request for the page. There are no analytics, no cookies and no accounts.

## What we never do
No ads. No selling data. No tracking.

## Kids
Keyrate isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
