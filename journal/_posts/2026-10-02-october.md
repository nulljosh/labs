## Oct 27

Curbfind is Scrounge now, live at scrounge.jaybulb.com; the store still says Curbfind.

Mailbag's Mac build reads Mail.app now, so Telus and other providers work. Airwell's iPhone build was rejected for a reason I can't read.

Patois passed dark mode and phone-width checks, with 128 courses. Icelus got transitions on the web and in Swift; its npm and registry launches wait on me.

Noema had its biggest day. I finished a Lean proof that Busy Beaver 2,3 is 38, then a C program counted every halting five-state Turing machine. Its count matches the published one; its halting times may be a new OEIS term, and 1,207 distinct runtimes may answer a 2020 question from Scott Aaronson. These are candidates, not breakthroughs; two OEIS drafts wait for me. In medicine, ClinicalTrials.gov baseline numbers show 407 standard deviations no arm size could produce, and 61 confidence intervals in 52 trials lost or gained a minus sign. Their p-values and two linked papers back the sign.

I emptied my Apple Notes into the right projects and uploaded animated App Store headers for 23 apps, not yet on store pages.

Wordroot became Eponym, a free -nym name. The store says Wordroot until the next update, the Mac app is a sidebar, and the share-sheet lookup and Watch word of the day ship next.

I sent 17 App Store headers to App Review and answered four spam rejections: Atlin and Horula on iPhone, Horula on Mac, and Oohaah on iPhone.
