## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree hit 2.0 with twenty-six apps in protected boxes; Bookrank is live offline on iPhone.

Premium is $1, auto-trading is live, and my paper rule hit 77%.

## Oct 4

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review.

Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign; iOS is live.

## Oct 5

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a logo on Samantha's face.

Apple rejected seven apps as spam and Hikko over screenshots.

NYC Survive's overnight loop made it winnable.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a cache fix.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi after I wrote the login handshake its chip lacked.

Samantha answers on the real Pi. Money comes from a Pi dev kit; the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote Joshua Tree's eleven-chapter book with board photos. I flashed the Pi card.

After a DNS fix it loaded heyitsmejosh.com over HTTPS; scripts don't run.

## Oct 11

I built xread, a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models. Most web apps left Supabase, so everyone resets passwords.

## Oct 12

Turing's picker clears all three dev bars; its C tool fires on 20 of 434 no-tool questions.

## Oct 13

I cut Joshua Tree's docs from fifty-six files to eleven and approved a plan to make the OS a daily driver. On the real card dragging still redraws everything. Turing's picker made 115 false calls; an abstain step is next.

## Oct 14

I built Noema on jaybulb.com.

## Oct 16

jaybulb.com had no mail records, so nothing sent there arrived; now every address works.

Talli's Stripe webhook rejected purchases since Oct 2; fixed.

## Oct 17

All 41 apps got a new brand kit and icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I fixed ten repos. Apple's rejections came from my lapsed domain, so I swapped hosts.

## Oct 18

Cruise fell from 16k XP a day to 11k on slow courses, so it picks only fast ones.

Atlin got an A+ icon, price drops and alerts.

Windows rework is done: four apps open at once, and a crash spares the rest. The Pi got admin accounts and a login screen.

Mailbag and Patois went into App Review; Quotestreak got a Daily Quote.
"Tell me more" works everywhere; stock prices came back after the old quote feed locked me out.
I cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31 and erased my plan folder from public history; only my vault has it.

## Oct 19

Quotestreak, now Coda, got a whitepaper, 14,000 Wikiquote lines.

Notate's big update went live on iPhone and Apple Watch, offline.

Glyphbox, now Icelus, draws a screen from one sentence; the web is live and the App Store build waits.

My litigation app is Temida now, in six languages, with a public repo after I scrubbed case details. Screenshots bounced the iPhone app, so I redid them with a made-up case; both are back in review.

Thinkpond went back to review, but it sucked, so it's Melete.

My portfolio is one ranked list of fifty projects, and I dropped 33 of 95 dead links.

On the Pi, holding Space talks to Samantha, who opens apps and fetches weather. I split Joshua Tree's biggest source file, cutting its test run from 46 minutes to 15.

I built Hermes, a Helm clone for App Store Connect that sends nothing without my yes.

## Oct 20

Nimble passes every BC grade 10 to 12 math, science and socials exam offline at A+, and Calculus I passes 120 of 120. New versions score low on first runs; I'd rather show that. Confident wrong word-problem answers fell from 46 to 2.

Talli's QA caught a wrong cheque date and a dead Sign Out on iPhone; both are fixed and in review.

I fixed Talli's Alberta earnings rule to the official $350 a month; apps keep your last numbers offline.

Epiphany got a blue bulb icon; all 45 sweep problems closed.

The calling app went through Liana, Marmoset, Irisa and Oohah, which another account owned, so it's Oohaah. Both builds are in App Review; calls work on cellular.

I renamed Notate to Echo. Its keyboard dictates into any app by opening Echo to record, since keyboards can't use the mic. Untested on iPhone; the store still says Notate.

Cartons, the UPS tracker, went into App Review on iPhone and Mac for 99 cents.

## Oct 21

Patois uses its new name everywhere, domain included, and a streak-freeze crash is fixed. Temida's Sign in with Apple fix is cleared on iPhone and Mac; Coda's App ID has the capability.

Intake was taken on the App Store, so the app is Machaon now. It has a stethoscope icon and a snake mascot called Aski. The native apps are leaving Supabase for my Cloudflare accounts, so web and phone share one account and the log syncs. Apple is reviewing it now.

The old icon shipped in the last Epiphany release, so I rebuilt the iPhone app and pulled the old one from review; the Mac build is in review. The web and phone disagreed about my money, so I fixed my income and wiped old debt. Web sign-in works again, any ticker can go on a watchlist, and stock pages show earnings and dividend dates for the iPhone calendar. Recurring charges show in statements; budget shows spend per line. The license is Apache 2.0, the privacy policy matches the app, and the landing ends with Prometheus, whose name means forethought.

I also found my money details in Epiphany's public code, so I moved them to private account data and rewrote history so no copy stays on a branch or tag. An old link still reaches one copy until GitHub purges it. Stripe now points at jaybulb.com, and open issues dropped from 133 to 67 today.

Curvely 2.0 went into App Review on iPhone and Mac with light mode, points, shaded inequalities. I said pick one; Claude chose Tangent, then Pan. Pan passed final QA; the store name waits.

NYC Survive is Gastown now. I cut needs, raids, weapons, quests and cash; you command a crew, build the Steam Clock, and let drills and generators run themselves. A headless bot called it unwinnable; now both bots win in twelve days. Both store builds are up, but Apple fails every screenshot, so nothing is in review.

I rebuilt the BC Garage Doors site to match bcgaragedoors.ca, with the same header, services, reviews, booking form and photos, and cut the price ranges and claims I had made up. It's live on bcgd.jaybulb.com. Then I cut the copy to one line per section and gave Specials, Tips and Blogs pages of their own. I also built a new Doorstock with parts, jobs and reorder emails, and sent the Mac version to Apple. Now we wait.
