Joshua Tree hit 2.0 with twenty-six apps sandboxed; Bookrank works offline on iPhone.

Premium is $1; auto-trading is live.

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review.

Apple rejected seven apps as spam, and Hikko over screenshots.

## Oct 6

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi once I wrote the login handshake its chip lacked; a dev kit pays for it, and the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote Joshua Tree's book and flashed the Pi card.

## Oct 11

I built a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models.

## Oct 13

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a logo on Samantha's face.

## Oct 16

Talli's Stripe webhook rejected purchases, now fixed; jaybulb.com had no mail records, now every address works.

## Oct 17

All 41 apps got a new brand kit and icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I fixed ten repos.

## Oct 18

Mailbag and Patois went into App Review, Quotestreak got a Daily Quote, and stocks came back.

I cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31 and erased my plan folder from public history.

## Oct 19

Notate's big update went live on iPhone and Watch, offline. Glyphbox became Icelus; the App Store build waits.

My litigation app is Temida now, with no case details in its public repo. Thinkpond became Melete.

I built Hermes, a Helm clone for App Store Connect that sends nothing without my yes.

I made my portfolio one ranked list of fifty projects and dropped 33 dead links.

## Oct 20

Nimble got A+ on every BC grade 10 to 12 math, science and socials exam offline; its confident wrong word-problem answers fell from 46 to 2.

Epiphany got a blue bulb icon and all 45 sweep problems closed. The calling app became Oohaah after three names; both builds are in review.

Notate became Echo; the store still says Notate.

Cartons went into App Review for 99 cents.

## Oct 21

Patois uses its new name everywhere; its streak-freeze crash is fixed. Temida's Sign in with Apple fix cleared on iPhone and Mac.

Intake was taken, so it's Machaon.

The web and phone disagreed about my money, so I fixed my income and cleared old debt.

Curvely 2.0 went into App Review as Pan.

NYC Survive is Gastown now: a crew, a Steam Clock, self-running drills, no raids or cash. A headless bot called it unwinnable, and now both win in twelve days.

I rebuilt the BC Garage Doors site without its made-up prices; Doorstock, with parts, jobs and reorder emails, went to Apple.

## Oct 22

Epiphany splits subscriptions by currency, so a US$200 plan stops hitting my Canadian totals.

My Duolingo runner is Surf now; a disk cleanup had killed it since Wednesday, so I moved it out of reach and saved the 197-day streak.

Apple rejected the first Cartons and Oohaah builds; Doorstock syncs shops and jobs.

## Oct 25

Joshua Tree's main source file shrank by half, and Samantha answers on the Pi without my Mac.

Coranto became Bugle, but Apple refused plain Bugle, so the store says BugleNews.

Clock became Horula, since every clock name was taken; by night it had twelve languages.

Five Apple appeals won; Atlin's store name is back.

## Oct 26

I cleared my Ja Gmail to zero with Mailbag: 14 filed, 4 archived.

I made Mirage, a desert platformer that Duke beat alone in 3,725 steps; its iPhone, iPad and Mac apps are building at mirage.jaybulb.com.

Patois's packs copy Duke's solved vocabulary, so Turkish went from 11 units to 120. I booked eight Product Hunt launches for Oct 28 to Nov 6 and appealed Cartons' iPhone spam rejection.

Joshua Tree 6.0 is in CI with a sixth tuft and a three-minute Samantha cap; my board-pass SD card is flashed.

Kaggle suspended my account without a reason, so training waits on my appeal.

## Oct 27

Curbfind became Scrounger, since Apple wouldn't take plain Scrounge; the store still says Curbfind.

Airwell's iPhone build was rejected for a reason I can't read.

Patois passed dark mode and phone-width checks at 128 courses. Icelus got transitions on web and Swift; its npm and registry launches wait on me.

Noema's biggest day: a Lean proof showed Busy Beaver 2,3 is 38, and a C program matched the published count of five-state halting machines. Its halting times may be a new OEIS term and may answer a 2020 Aaronson question, though they are candidates and two OEIS drafts wait for me. In medicine, ClinicalTrials.gov has 407 impossible standard deviations and 61 confidence intervals in 52 trials from a lost minus sign.

## Oct 28

Apple cleared my Sidewise spam answers but one iPad error from a dead server, so I took it off sale and folded it into Bugle.

Bugle's iPhone and Mac builds are live, with a newer Mac build in review. Epiphany's news thumbnails shipped, and a second feed, MarketWatch, took its stories from 25 to 84.

Wordroot became Eponym, a free -nym name; the store still says Wordroot.

Homeward became Sighting, my lost-pets app: a species filter, maps and a photo-first post flow on web, phone and Mac, running on my own API, with pet alerts for nearby posts.

Scrounger got category chips, saved searches, a map and price-drop badges on web, iPhone and Mac. I made commercials for both apps.

Kernel went into App Review at lunchtime, then its loop ran until night: a storage screen, a fading sleep timer, a shake that adds ten minutes, and text that follows the largest size. It narrates in French, Spanish and German, and first-run pages offer Goodreads sign-in.

I sent 17 App Store headers to review and answered four spam rejections: Atlin, Horula and Oohaah.

Duke's Oxygen Not Included autopilot ate my afternoon: five colonies died by cycle 31, and the sixth, at cycle 9, runs without algae.

## Oct 29

Placid Platypus went from known through 176 to 181, with about 80 new witness machines, each rerun from a blank tape. Witnesses come in adjacent pairs, so a gap is a count nothing straddles, and searching the machines beside it cracked 177, 178 and 188 after brute force failed. I opened the AI lane, the eleventh in the lab, and found only 13 of 182 SWE-bench Verified submissions list every task, six of those miss some, and 169 cannot be checked. I machine-checked the weakest step of the triangular Ising proof for n = 2 to 6, and it matched two published numbers. Meanwhile 182 has resisted five hours, and the Mac stopped filling its disk once Claude's 8-gigabyte transcripts moved to the LaCie.
