## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree hit 2.0 with twenty-six apps in protected boxes; Bookrank works offline on iPhone.

Premium is $1, auto-trading is live, and my paper rule hit 77%.

## Oct 4

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review.

Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign; iOS is live.

## Oct 5

Apple rejected seven apps as spam and Hikko over screenshots.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a cache fix.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi after I wrote the login handshake its chip lacked. Money comes from a Pi dev kit; the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote Joshua Tree's eleven-chapter book with board photos and flashed the Pi card.

## Oct 11

I built a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models.

## Oct 13

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a logo on Samantha's face.

## Oct 16

Talli's Stripe webhook rejected purchases; fixed. jaybulb.com had no mail records, so nothing arrived; now every address works.

## Oct 17

All 41 apps got a new brand kit and icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I fixed ten repos. Apple's rejections came from my lapsed domain, so I swapped hosts.

## Oct 18

Joshua Tree's windows rework is done: four apps open at once, and a crash spares the rest. Cruise fell from 16k XP a day to 11k on slow courses, so it picks fast ones.

Mailbag and Patois went into App Review, Quotestreak got a Daily Quote, and stock prices came back.

I cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31 and erased my plan folder from public history.

## Oct 19

Notate's big update went live on iPhone and Watch, offline. Glyphbox became Icelus; the App Store build waits.

My litigation app is Temida now, with a public repo after I scrubbed case details. Thinkpond became Melete.

On the Pi, holding Space talks to Samantha, who opens apps and fetches weather. I built Hermes, a Helm clone for App Store Connect that sends nothing without my yes.

My portfolio is one ranked list of fifty projects, and I dropped 33 of 95 dead links.

## Oct 20

Nimble passes every BC grade 10 to 12 math, science and socials exam offline at A+, and its confident wrong word-problem answers fell from 46 to 2.

Epiphany got a blue bulb icon and all 45 sweep problems closed. Talli's QA caught a wrong cheque date and a dead Sign Out; both are fixed.
The calling app went through three names, ending as Oohaah, which another account owned; both builds are in review.

I renamed Notate to Echo; the store still says Notate.

Cartons went into App Review for 99 cents.

## Oct 21

Patois uses its new name everywhere, and its streak-freeze crash is fixed. Temida's Sign in with Apple fix cleared on iPhone and Mac.

Intake was taken on the App Store, so it's Machaon. Its native apps moved from Supabase to Cloudflare, so web and phone share one account.

Epiphany's Mac build is in review. The web and phone disagreed about my money, so I fixed my income, wiped old debt and scrubbed it from public history.

Curvely 2.0 went into App Review on iPhone and Mac. Claude named it Tangent, then Pan; the store name waits.

NYC Survive is Gastown now: a crew, a Steam Clock and self-running drills, no raids or cash. A headless bot called it unwinnable; now both win in twelve days.

I rebuilt the BC Garage Doors site to match bcgaragedoors.ca, minus its made-up prices. Doorstock, with parts, jobs and reorder emails, went to Apple for Mac.

## Oct 22

Epiphany splits subscriptions by currency, so a US$200 plan stops landing in my Canadian totals.
My Duolingo runner is Surf now and open source. A disk cleanup killed it from Wednesday; I moved it out of cleanup's reach and saved the 197-day streak.

Apple rejected the first Cartons and Oohaah builds. Doorstock syncs shops, crews and jobs, garage door leads left Supabase.

Unreal kept reopening at login because of a leftover Epic launcher agent. It's off and locked, but I won't trust it until reboot.

## Oct 25

Joshua Tree's main source file shrank by over half, and Samantha now answers on the Pi without my Mac.

I renamed Coranto to Bugle. Apple refused plain Bugle, so the store name is BugleNews, and both submissions were pulled.
Epiphany's news now comes from Yahoo Finance and CNBC, so it's never empty.

Clock became Horula, since every clock name was taken. By night it had twelve languages, a day and night map and an embeddable clock.

I took Hermes from 4.0 to 10.0 that afternoon: an app grid, an iPad split view, release timelines, Xcode Cloud runs and a keyword audit.

Five Apple appeals were confirmed, and Atlin's store name is back.

Melete and Machaon's Mac builds failed review on sign-in snags; both are fixed and resubmitted.

## Oct 26

I cleared my Ja Gmail to zero with Mailbag, filing 14 mails and archiving 4 without deleting any.

I made Mirage, a desert platformer built from scratch. It began as a port of a fan game, until I asked for our own art and story. Wren climbs from the town of Dry Creek through Red Canyon, the Cholla Caves and the Salt Flats to beat the Dust King and bring the spring back. It runs in the browser at mirage.jaybulb.com, and the iPhone, iPad and Mac apps build now, with a 20-second ad. Duke beat it to the end on its own in 3,725 steps. The next Joshua Tree build carries it on the PC and the Pi, with its PR pending CI. The App Store record waits until tomorrow, since Apple is throttling my sign-ins.

Echo's keyboard is with Apple on iPhone and Mac, untested on a phone. Custom words fix transcripts too.

Bugle's Home got rebuilt with collections, a podcast shelf, unread dots and a topic picker. Its mascot, a round-faced horn that toots on launch, is the app icon now.

Horula finished its rivals' feature list; it waits on Apple.

Duke, my Duolingo runner (ex-Surf), also plays FEZ alone, 24 of 32 cube bits so far.

Patois's language packs copy Duke's solved vocabulary, so Turkish went from 11 units to 120. I booked eight Product Hunt launches for Oct 28 to Nov 6, fixed old pages still saying Inkpress, Quotestreak or NYC Survive, and wrote X threads for five, none posted yet.
I appealed Cartons' spam rejection on iPhone from Safari, a day after the Mac one.

Joshua Tree 6.0 is in CI with a sixth tuft on the logo, personal wallpapers and a three-minute cap on Samantha checks. It merges on green, which clears my no-open-issues goal, and my board-pass SD card is flashed.
