## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree hit 2.0 with twenty-six apps in protected boxes; Bookrank is live offline on iPhone.

Premium is $1, auto-trading is live, and my paper-traded Double 7s rule hit 77 percent.

## Oct 4

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review.

Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign; iOS is live.

## Oct 5

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a logo on Samantha's face.

Apple rejected seven apps as spam and Hikko over screenshots.

NYC Survive's overnight loop made it winnable; iOS is live, macOS in review.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a cache fix.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi after I wrote the login handshake its chip lacked.

Samantha answers on the real Pi. Money comes from a Pi dev kit; the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote Joshua Tree's eleven-chapter book with board photos. I halved my custom skills and flashed the Pi card.

After a DNS fix it loaded heyitsmejosh.com over HTTPS; scripts don't run.

## Oct 11

I built xread, a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models. Most web apps left Supabase, so everyone resets passwords. Heyitsmejosh.com lapses on the 13th.

## Oct 12

Turing's picker clears all three dev bars, and its C tool fires on only 20 of 434 no-tool questions.

I folded ten Pi pull requests into one and flashed the card, but multi-window is broken.

## Oct 13

I cut Joshua Tree's docs from fifty-six files to eleven and approved a plan to make the OS a daily driver. On the real card my mouse survives reboot, but dragging redraws everything. Turing's picker made 115 false calls; a PR adds an abstain step.

## Oct 14

I built Noema on jaybulb.com; Lean checked one small proof. I merged fifty-seven Apple Notes into twenty-three.

## Oct 16

jaybulb.com had no mail records, so nothing sent there arrived; now every address reaches me.

Reset emails carry each app's name, and Quotestreak got a forgot-password page. Talli's Stripe webhook rejected purchases since Oct 2; fixed.

My portfolio moved to jaybulb.com; App Store apps keep the old address until the next build.

## Oct 17

Tonchi is Patois, Hikko is Thinkpond and Windgate is Airwell. All 41 apps got a new brand kit and icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I patched ten repos. Apple's rejections came from builds calling my lapsed domain, so I swapped hosts.

Unreal's limit is RAM, so it gets one day a week until a 1 TB SSD lands and the LaCie goes back to Dad.

## Oct 18

Cruise fell from 16k XP a day to 11k on slow courses, so it picks only fast ones.

Atlin got an A+ icon, plus price drops, alerts, map search, walk scores and open houses.

Turing can now edit video and make 3D files.

Joshua Tree was red because its checks hit my lapsed domain. Windows rework is done: four apps open at once, and a crash spares the rest. The Pi got admin accounts and a login screen.

Mailbag and Patois went into App Review, and Quotestreak got a Daily Quote. Dave, a friend of my dad's, bought Blockprint.

"Tell me more" works everywhere, and stock prices came back after the old quote feed locked me out. Nim went onto Android and desktop in nine languages.

I cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31 and erased my plan folder from public history; only my vault has it. The Mac mini's disk kept filling, so the model cache went to the LaCie.

Tripwire's fixer had never opened a PR. Now a red main sends Claude Code to draft a fix; the first fixed a Quotestreak test.

## Oct 19

Quotestreak, now Coda, got a whitepaper, public roadmap and 14,000 Wikiquote lines.

Notate's big update went live on iPhone and Apple Watch, offline.

Nimble's offline pack grew to 14,000 Wikipedia articles, and Pre-Calc 12 works offline too.

Glyphbox, now Icelus, went from text tables to a design tool in a day. Layers, flowcharts, masks, auto layout and a chat panel that draws a screen from one sentence. The web is live; the App Store build waits on Apple.

My litigation app is Temida now, and speaks six languages. I made its repo public after scrubbing case details. The iPhone app bounced over sign-in-only screenshots, so I redid them with a made-up case; both are back in review.

Thinkpond went back to review. I decided it kind of sucked, so it's Melete now, after the Muse of thought.

My jaybulb.com portfolio is now one list of fifty projects, ranked by how far each shipped. I dropped 33 of 95 dead links.

I added Hermes 3 beside Qwen, the default. Qwen wrote better code; Hermes was faster and better in chat but botched git rebase.

On the Pi, holding Space talks to Samantha, who opens any Spotlight app and fetches weather. I split Joshua Tree's biggest source file and cut its test run from 46 minutes to 15.

I wrote update and disk-check commands; brew doctor is clean.

I built Hermes, a Helm clone for App Store Connect, in blue with a privacy page. The iPhone app answers reviews once I confirm each one. Resolve All reads every open rejection and drafts each reply and GitHub issue with a free local model. Nothing sends until I check it. The notarized Mac release installs with Homebrew.

## Oct 20

Mailbag got a Hermes-style landing and blue icons, but the store build still has pink. It cleared my mail: iCloud at zero, Gmail too.

Nimble now passes every BC grade 10 to 12 math, science and socials exam offline at A+, on web, Apple and Kotlin. Calculus I, my first university course, passes 120 of 120 offline. New versions still score low on first runs, and I'd rather show that. "What's your name" gets the right answer, and confident wrong word-problem answers fell from 46 to 2.

Epiphany got a blue bulb icon, its own font and bouncy pins. I closed 43 of 45 sweep problems (bigger taps, no endless spinners), and the web is live. Then I gave it an Apollo theme like Hermes: a lyre icon, laurel-leaf quote marks, sans serif headlines and a brand kit page. I kept Epiphany over a Greek god rename. iPhone and Mac are in Apple's review now.

The calling app went through Liana, Marmoset and Irisa, then Oohah, which another Apple account owned, so it's Oohaah, with Momo, a winged monkey, as the mascot. It has a block list, a chat filter and an 18-plus gate for Apple's safety rules, and the iPhone and Mac apps share one lobby with the web. A practice call with Momo fills the wait when nobody's online. It's Liquid Glass with no gradients. The App Store record is ready and builds are uploading; review is next.
