## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree hit 2.0 with twenty-six apps in protected boxes, and Bookrank is live on iPhone offline.

Premium is $1, auto-trading is live, and my paper-traded Double 7s rule hit 77 percent.

## Oct 4

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review.

Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign; iOS is live.

## Oct 5

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a logo on Samantha's face.

Apple rejected seven apps as spam and Hikko over screenshots.

NYC Survive's overnight loop made it winnable; iOS is live, macOS in review.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a cache flush fix.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi after I wrote the login handshake its chip lacked.

Samantha answers on the real Pi. Money comes from a Pi dev kit; the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote Joshua Tree's eleven-chapter book with board photos.

After a DNS fix it loaded heyitsmejosh.com over HTTPS; scripts don't run.

## Oct 11

I built xread, a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models. Most web apps left Supabase, so everyone resets passwords. Heyitsmejosh.com lapses on the 13th.

## Oct 12

Turing's picker clears all three dev bars, and its C tool fires on only 20 of 434 no-tool questions.

I folded ten Pi pull requests into one and flashed the card. It boots and the mouse reconnects, but multi-window is broken.

## Oct 13

I cut Joshua Tree's docs from fifty-six files to eleven and approved a plan to make the OS a daily driver. On the real card my mouse survives reboot, but dragging redraws everything. Turing's picker made 115 false calls; a PR adds an abstain step.

## Oct 14

I built Noema on jaybulb.com; Lean checked one small proof.

## Oct 16

jaybulb.com had no mail records, so nothing sent there could arrive; now every address reaches me.

Reset emails carry each app's name, and Quotestreak got a forgot-password page. Talli's Stripe webhook had rejected purchases since Oct 2, now fixed.

My portfolio moved to jaybulb.com; App Store apps keep the old address until their next build.

## Oct 17

Tonchi is Patois, Hikko is Thinkpond and Windgate is Airwell. All 41 apps got a new brand kit and icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I patched ten repos. Apple's rejections came from builds calling my lapsed domain, so I swapped hosts.

Unreal's limit is RAM, so it gets one day a week until a 1 TB SSD lands and the LaCie goes back to Dad.

## Oct 18

Cruise fell from 16k XP a day to 11k on slow courses, so it picks only fast ones.

Atlin got an A+ icon, plus price drops, alerts, map search, walk scores and open houses.

Turing can edit video, make GIFs, trim sound and make Blender 3D files.

Joshua Tree was red because its checks hit my lapsed domain. Windows rework is done: four apps open at once, and a crash spares the rest. The Pi got admin accounts and a login screen.

Mailbag and Patois went into App Review, and Quotestreak got a Daily Quote. Blockprint's third version shipped as one app. Dave, a friend of my dad's, bought it.

"Tell me more" works everywhere, and stock prices came back after the old quote feed locked me out. Nim went onto Android and desktop in nine languages.

I cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31 and erased my plan folder from the public repo's history; only my vault has it. The Mac mini's disk kept filling, so the model cache went to the LaCie.

Tripwire's fixer had never opened a PR. Now a red main sends Claude Code to draft a fix; the first fixed a Quotestreak test.

## Oct 19

Quotestreak, now Coda, got a whitepaper, a public roadmap, and 14,000 Wikiquote lines.

I cut features nobody asked for and slowed sign-in.

Notate's big update went live on iPhone and Apple Watch, offline from first open.

Nimble's offline pack grew to 14,000 Wikipedia articles and passes 72 of 154 test questions; wrong answers fell from 49 to 3. Its Pre-Calc 12 solver runs offline too, passing 279 of 281 practice questions.

Glyphbox, now Icelus, went from text tables to a real design tool in a day. I added layers, flowcharts, masks, auto layout, and a chat panel that draws a screen from one sentence. The iPhone version got glass bars and a tab bar. The web is live; the App Store build waits on Apple.

My litigation app is Temida now, with its own serif and logo, and it speaks six languages. I made its repo public after scrubbing case details. The iPhone app bounced over sign-in-only screenshots, so I redid them with a made-up case; both are back in review.

Thinkpond went back to App Review, then I decided it kind of sucked, so it's Melete now, after the Muse of thought.

My jaybulb.com portfolio is now one list of fifty projects, ranked by how far each has shipped. I checked all 95 links and dropped 33 dead addresses.

I added Hermes 3 beside Qwen, the default. Qwen wrote better code in my tests; Hermes was faster and better in chat but botched git rebase.

On the Pi, holding Space talks to Samantha, who opens any app Spotlight can and fetches the weather. I split Joshua Tree's biggest source file and cut its test run from 46 minutes to 15.

I wrote two commands that run every update and check the disk. Brew doctor now comes back clean.

I built Hermes, a Helm clone for App Store Connect. It got build uploads, light mode, a blue brand and a rebuilt font. I restyled its landing and added docs and a privacy page. The iPhone app talks straight to Apple and answers reviews once I confirm each one. Resolve All reads every open rejection and has a free local model draft each reply and GitHub issue. Nothing sends until I check every line. The notarized Mac release installs with Homebrew.

## Oct 20

I refreshed the labs repo and fixed old app names in its roadmap. Mailbag's landing got a Hermes-style rebuild and its icons went blue, but the store build has the pink one. I ran it on my mail: iCloud at zero, Gmail cleared.

Nimble's offline course packs kept coming, and 20th Century World History and Law Studies each pass 120 of 120 offline everywhere. Fresh-form first runs still score low, and I'd rather show that. Epiphany got a blue bulb icon and its own font, and its map pins spring in and bounce when tapped. The new build is ready and waits on my yes to go to Apple. The portfolio's list now syncs with my repos before each deploy, and its footer fades into the landscape.
