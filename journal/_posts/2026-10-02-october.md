Noema had its biggest day yet. I finished a Lean proof that Busy Beaver 2,3 is 38, then a C program counted every halting five-state Turing machine. Its count matches the published one, the halting times may be a new OEIS term, and the same run found 1,207 distinct runtimes, maybe an answer to a question Scott Aaronson asked in 2020. These are candidates, not breakthroughs, and two OEIS drafts wait for me. In medicine, ClinicalTrials.gov baseline numbers show 407 standard deviations no arm size could produce, and 61 confidence intervals in 52 trials lost or gained a minus sign. Their own p-values back the sign, and two linked papers print it right.

I emptied my Apple Notes into the right projects. I also made animated App Store headers for 23 apps and uploaded them. They aren't on store pages yet, and Apple hasn't looked at them.
