## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree hit 2.0 with twenty-six apps in protected boxes, and Bookrank is live on iPhone with natural voices and offline reading.

Premium is $1, auto-trading is live, and my paper-traded Double 7s rule hit 77 percent. Notate is live on iPhone.

## Oct 4

A Pi 4B is coming for hardware tests.

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review.

Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign; iOS is live.

## Oct 5

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a logo on Samantha's face.

Apple rejected seven apps as spam and Hikko over screenshots.

NYC Survive's overnight loop made it winnable; iOS is live, macOS in review.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a cache flush fix.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi after I wrote the login handshake its chip lacked.

Samantha answers on the real Pi, and my Mac relay picks a cheap or strong model per question. Money comes from a Pi dev kit; the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote Joshua Tree's eleven-chapter book with board photos.

After a DNS fix it loaded heyitsmejosh.com over HTTPS; scripts still don't run. Every Pi image carried my relay key; the fix is in review.

## Oct 9 to 10

I halved my custom skills and flashed the Pi card.

## Oct 11

I built xread, a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models. Most web apps moved off Supabase, so everyone resets passwords. Heyitsmejosh.com lapses on the 13th; the brand moves to jaybulb.com.

Four repos took new names, eleven App Store listings moved to jaybulb.com, twenty Joshua Tree changes merged, and my first Pi 4 run booted to a desktop.

## Oct 12

Turing's picker clears all three dev bars, and its C tool fires on only 20 of 434 no-tool questions.

I folded ten Pi pull requests into one and flashed the card. It boots and the mouse reconnects, but multi-window is broken and the boot bar is choppy.

## Oct 13

I cut Joshua Tree's docs from fifty-six files to eleven and approved a plan to make the OS a daily driver. On the real card my mouse survives reboot, but dragging redraws the whole screen. Turing's picker made 115 false calls; an abstain step fixes that, PR open.

## Oct 14

I built Noema on jaybulb.com, and Lean checked one small proof.

## Oct 15

I merged fifty-seven Apple Notes into twenty-three.

## Oct 16

I filed 33 of 34 Apple Notes into roadmaps, then lost a day to updates.

jaybulb.com had no mail records, so nothing sent there could arrive; now every address reaches me.

Reset emails carry each app's name, and Quotestreak got a forgot-password page. Hikko's sign-in hung on my dead domain, so I moved it. Talli's Stripe webhook had rejected purchases since Oct 2, now fixed.

My portfolio moved to jaybulb.com, and App Store apps keep the old address until the next build. Wordroot's homepage is now the app in Apple style; I dropped its font for looking like Pokemon.

## Oct 17

Tonchi is Patois, Hikko is Thinkpond and Windgate is Airwell. All 41 apps got a brand kit and a new icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I patched about ten repos. Apple's rejections came from builds calling my lapsed domain, so I swapped hosts. Thinkpond is back in App Review; Mailbag, Epiphany and Intake need new builds.

Unreal's limit is RAM, not skill: the editor took 16 minutes to boot from the LaCie, then ran out of memory. Unreal gets one day a week until a 1 TB SSD lands and the LaCie goes back to Dad.

## Oct 18

Cruise slowed from 16k XP a day to 11k by picking courses it was furthest behind on, even slow ones. Now a course must be fast enough to earn a pick.

I finished How AI Thinks, cleared my Books folder and added a Fiend summary. Both are live in Bookrank.

Atlin got a folded-map-and-pin icon I gave an A+. The build in review adds Sign in with Apple, account deletion and synced saves. Since then I've added price drops, alerts, draw-on-map search, walk scores, price history and open houses. Zillow and REW.ca are on, so the web shows real rentals and sales; US sale is Zillow and Craigslist only.

Turing can cut, join, mute and speed up videos, make GIFs, pull and trim sound, and make 3D files in Blender. I gave her a garage glyph for a garage door logo and moved her to turing.jaybulb.com.

Joshua Tree was red because its checks fetched Samantha's face from my lapsed domain; fixed. The windows rework is done: four apps open at once, and a crash spares the rest. On the Pi I added admin accounts and a login screen.

Mailbag and Patois went into App Review, and Quotestreak got a Daily Quote, decades, hints, real difficulty and friends. Blockprint's third version shipped as one app on every device. Dave, a friend of my dad's, bought it, my first real sale, and I posted it on r/SideProject and X. Blackcomb got a Safari-style start page and mountain icon.

Everyday math and physical constants work offline. "Tell me more" works on every platform, and stock prices came back after the old quote feed locked me out. Nim went onto Android and desktop in nine languages.

My X reader's digest gave two ideas: a file size check for AI and a show-me tool.

I filed nine more Apple Notes into roadmaps and the wiki, and folded GitHub from 62 repos to 31: twenty-five small projects are folders in one repo, four private ones went into vault. I also erased my plan folder from the public repo's history; it lives only in my vault. The Mac mini's disk kept filling down to about 5 GB, so I cleared caches and moved the local model's 5.4 GB cache to the LaCie. About 27 GB is free now, and the model only runs with the LaCie plugged in.

I audited Tripwire, which files an issue when CI goes red and closes it on green. Its fixer had never opened a pull request, so now a red main starts Claude Code in GitHub Actions to fix the cause and open a draft, or explain on the issue when the problem isn't code. The first fixed a Quotestreak test and closed its own issue.

## Oct 19

Quotestreak got The Book, a whitepaper and a public roadmap. Quotestreak 100 shipped: a bank past 14,000 Wikiquote lines, a birthday hat for Quip, and fair play on both banks. The game is now called Coda.

I built features nobody asked for and was slow on sign-in, so I cut the live show, the weekly cup, clubs, champions, the hall of fame, the newsletter and stream mode.

Another session's builds filled the Mac mini's disk once, no data lost.
