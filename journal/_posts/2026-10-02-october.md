## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree hit 2.0 with twenty-six apps in protected boxes; Bookrank is live offline on iPhone.

Premium is $1, auto-trading is live, and my paper rule hit 77 percent.

## Oct 4

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review.

Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign; iOS is live.

## Oct 5

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a logo on Samantha's face.

Apple rejected seven apps as spam and Hikko over screenshots.

NYC Survive's overnight loop made it winnable; iOS is live, macOS in review.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a cache fix.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi after I wrote the login handshake its chip lacked.

Samantha answers on the real Pi. Money comes from a Pi dev kit; the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote Joshua Tree's eleven-chapter book with board photos. I flashed the Pi card.

After a DNS fix it loaded heyitsmejosh.com over HTTPS; scripts don't run.

## Oct 11

I built xread, a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models. Most web apps left Supabase, so everyone resets passwords.

## Oct 12

Turing's picker clears all three dev bars; its C tool fires on 20 of 434 no-tool questions.

## Oct 13

I cut Joshua Tree's docs from fifty-six files to eleven and approved a plan to make the OS a daily driver. On the real card my mouse survives reboot, but dragging redraws everything. Turing's picker made 115 false calls; an abstain step is next.

## Oct 14

I built Noema on jaybulb.com; Lean checked a small proof.

## Oct 16

jaybulb.com had no mail records, so nothing sent there arrived; now every address works.

Talli's Stripe webhook rejected purchases since Oct 2; fixed.

My portfolio moved to jaybulb.com.

## Oct 17

Tonchi is Patois, Hikko is Thinkpond and Windgate is Airwell. All 41 apps got a new brand kit and icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I fixed ten repos. Apple's rejections came from my lapsed domain, so I swapped hosts.

Unreal gets one day a week until a 1 TB SSD lands; RAM is its limit.

## Oct 18

Cruise fell from 16k XP a day to 11k on slow courses, so it picks only fast ones.

Atlin got an A+ icon, price drops and alerts.

Joshua Tree was red because its checks hit my old domain. Windows rework is done: four apps open at once, and a crash spares the rest. The Pi got admin accounts and a login screen.

Mailbag and Patois went into App Review, and Quotestreak got a Daily Quote. Dave, my dad's friend, bought Blockprint.

"Tell me more" works everywhere; stock prices came back after the old quote feed locked me out.
I cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31 and erased my plan folder from public history; only my vault has it.

## Oct 19

Quotestreak, now Coda, got a whitepaper, 14,000 Wikiquote lines.

Notate's big update went live on iPhone and Apple Watch, offline.

Glyphbox, now Icelus, became a design tool that draws a screen from one sentence. The web is live; the App Store build waits.

My litigation app is Temida now, in six languages, with a public repo after I scrubbed case details. The iPhone app bounced over screenshots, so I redid them with a made-up case. Both are back in review.

Thinkpond went back to review, but it kind of sucked, so it's Melete.

My portfolio is one ranked list of fifty projects, and I dropped 33 of 95 dead links.

I added Hermes 3 beside Qwen, which stays the default; Qwen wrote better code, Hermes botched a git rebase.

On the Pi, holding Space talks to Samantha, who opens apps and fetches weather. I split Joshua Tree's biggest source file and cut its test run from 46 minutes to 15.

I built Hermes, a Helm clone for App Store Connect that sends nothing until I confirm it.

## Oct 20

Mailbag got a new landing, but the store build is pink.

Nimble passes every BC grade 10 to 12 math, science and socials exam offline at A+, and Calculus I passes 120 of 120. New versions score low on first runs; I'd rather show that. Confident wrong word-problem answers fell from 46 to 2.

Talli's QA caught a wrong cheque date and a dead Sign Out on iPhone; both are fixed and in Apple's review on iPhone and Mac.

I fixed Talli's Alberta earnings rule to the official $350 a month; apps keep your last numbers when the signal drops.

Epiphany got a blue bulb icon, its own font and bouncy pins, and all 45 sweep problems closed. Then an Apollo theme, same name. Both are in Apple review.

The calling app went through Liana, Marmoset, Irisa and Oohah, which another account owned, so it's Oohaah. Both builds are in App Review; calls work on cellular.

I renamed Notate back to Echo. Its keyboard dictates into any app by opening Echo to record, since keyboards can't use the mic. I haven't tried it on a real iPhone yet, and the store still says Notate.

Cruise, my Duolingo autoplayer, sat dead from 1:20 to 10:55, but the streak held at 195.

Cartons, my UPS tracker, went into App Review on iPhone and Mac for 99 cents.

## Oct 21

Patois now uses its new name everywhere, domain included, and a streak-freeze crash is fixed. Epiphany learns a category per merchant, so one fix sticks across every statement. Temida's Sign in with Apple token mismatch is cleared on iPhone and Mac, and Coda's App ID now has Sign In with Apple.

Curvely 2.0 went into App Review on iPhone and Mac, with light mode, points, shaded inequalities and angle-drawn curves. Its landing now opens on the live app. On the web I added functions, lists, sliders and 85 one-tap patterns. I said pick one; Claude chose Tangent, then Pan, since an app named Tangent already exists. Pan passed final QA; the store name waits on 2.0's review.

NYC Survive is Gastown now, and it took most of my day. It has its own repo, a Vancouver setting, and new original sprites and a font. I cut needs, raids, weapons, quests and cash. You command a crew, drills and generators run on their own, and you win by building the Steam Clock. Web and native play by the same rules, and both bots win in about twelve days. A headless bot had shown it couldn't be won, and New Game dropped from a 7.7 second freeze to 0.05. The 2.0 build is in App Store Connect but not submitted, so the store still says NYC Survive.

Icelus's newest build is live with a contrast check, and Cruise's Arabic fix is in with its runner back up. Curbfind gets no watch or vision app.
