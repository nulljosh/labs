## Oct 19

Quotestreak, now Coda, got a whitepaper, 14,000 Wikiquote lines.

Notate's big update went live on iPhone and Apple Watch, offline.

Glyphbox, now Icelus, draws a screen from one sentence; the App Store build waits.

I built Hermes, a Helm clone for App Store Connect that sends nothing without my yes.

## Oct 20

Nimble passes every BC grade 10 to 12 math, science and socials exam offline at A+. New versions score low on first runs; I'd rather show that. Confident wrong word-problem answers fell from 46 to 2.

Talli's QA caught a wrong cheque date and a dead Sign Out on iPhone; both are fixed and in review.

I fixed Talli's Alberta earnings rule to the official $350 a month; apps keep your last numbers offline.

Epiphany got a blue bulb icon; all 45 sweep problems closed.

The calling app went through Liana, Marmoset, Irisa and Oohah, which another account owned, so it's Oohaah. Both builds are in App Review; calls work on cellular.

I renamed Notate to Echo. Its keyboard dictates into any app by opening Echo to record, since keyboards can't use the mic. Untested on iPhone; the store still says Notate.

Cartons, the UPS tracker, went into App Review on iPhone and Mac for 99 cents.

## Oct 21

Patois uses its new name everywhere, and a streak-freeze crash is fixed. Temida's Sign in with Apple fix is cleared on iPhone and Mac.

Intake was taken on the App Store, so the app is Machaon. The native apps moved off Supabase to Cloudflare, so web and phone share one account. Apple is reviewing it.

I rebuilt the iPhone app; the Mac build is in review. The web and phone disagreed about my money, so I fixed my income and wiped old debt. The landing ends on Prometheus, meaning forethought.

I also found my money details in Epiphany's public code, moved them to private data, and rewrote history so no copy stays on a branch or tag. An old link still reaches one copy until GitHub purges it. Stripe now points at jaybulb.com, and open issues dropped from 133 to 67 today.

Curvely 2.0 went into App Review on iPhone and Mac with light mode, points and shaded inequalities. Claude named it Tangent, then Pan; the store name waits.

NYC Survive is Gastown now. I cut needs, raids, weapons, quests and cash; you command a crew, build the Steam Clock, and let drills and generators run themselves. A headless bot called it unwinnable; now both bots win in twelve days. Both store builds are up, but Apple fails every screenshot, so nothing is in review.

I rebuilt the BC Garage Doors site to match bcgaragedoors.ca and cut the made-up price ranges and claims. It's live on bcgd.jaybulb.com. I also built a new Doorstock with parts, jobs and reorder emails, sent the Mac version to Apple, and added a Today screen for techs while it waits.

## Oct 22

Epiphany splits subscriptions by currency, so a US$200 plan stops landing in my Canadian totals. I QA'd its mobile web and the apps now read Fear & Greed from CNN because my server is blocked. Dull symbol avatars got swapped for 108 Greek god busts.

Apple rejected the first Cartons and Oohaah builds; I'll read why once sign-in stops throttling.

Machaon's sign-in got the landing's plain look, Doorstock syncs shops, crews and jobs, and the garage door site stopped sending leads through Supabase.

My Duolingo runner is Surf now, and it's open source. A disk cleanup wiped the build folder its launcher used, so it was dead from Wednesday. I moved the app out of cleanup's reach and saved the 197-day streak. Its font drew waves in every comma; fixed.
I dug up my Pre-Calc 12 masterclass, added the missing conic sections, log equations and unit circle, brought the web copy to ten units, and made a PDF. I put my honest 50 to 70 percent estimate in the study guide.
