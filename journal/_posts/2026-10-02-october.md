## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree reached 2.0 with twenty-six apps in protected boxes. Bookrank is live on iPhone with natural voices and offline reading.

Premium is $1 and auto-trading is live. My paper-traded Double 7s rule hit 77 percent. Notate is live on iPhone.

## Oct 4

A Pi 4B is coming for hardware tests.

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review.

Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign; iOS is live.


## Oct 5

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a refreshed logo on Samantha's face.

Apple rejected seven apps as spam and Hikko over screenshots.


NYC Survive's overnight loop made it winnable; iOS is live, macOS in review.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a cache flush fix.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi after I wrote the login handshake its chip lacked.

Samantha answers on the real Pi on my API credit. My Mac relay picks a cheap or strong model per question and gives her read-only access to one folder. Money comes from a Pi dev kit; the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote Joshua Tree's eleven-chapter book with board photos.

After a DNS fix it loaded heyitsmejosh.com over HTTPS; scripts still don't run. Every Pi image carried my relay key; the fix is in review.

## Oct 9 to 10

I halved my custom skills and flashed the Pi card.

## Oct 11

I built xread, a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models. Most web apps moved off Supabase, so everyone resets passwords. Heyitsmejosh.com lapses on the 13th; the brand moves to jaybulb.com.

Turing can drive Joshua Tree from chat on her build machines only.


Four repos took new names and eleven App Store listings moved to jaybulb.com. 
Twenty Joshua Tree changes merged, and my first Pi 4 run boots to a desktop. Bluetooth finds my mouse but hangs up before pairing.

## Oct 12

Turing's picker clears all three dev bars, and its C tool fires on only 20 of 434 no-tool questions. The sealed sets stay unread.

I folded ten Pi pull requests into one and flashed the card. It boots, and the mouse reconnects by itself. Multi-window is broken, the boot bar is choppy and the wallpaper is terracotta.

## Oct 13

I cut Joshua Tree's docs from fifty-six files to eleven. I approved a plan to make the OS a daily driver. On the real card my mouse survives reboot, but dragging redraws the whole screen. Turing's picker made 115 false calls against a bar of 22; an abstain step fixes that, PR open.


## Oct 14

I built Noema on jaybulb.com. First-descent checks passed past a million, and Lean checked one small proof.


## Oct 15

I synced sixty skills into Codex and merged fifty-seven Apple Notes into twenty-three.

## Oct 16

I filed 33 of 34 Apple Notes into roadmaps, then lost a day to updates.

jaybulb.com had no mail records, so nothing sent there could arrive. Now every address reaches my inbox.

Reset emails now carry each app's name. Quotestreak got a forgot-password page.

Hikko's sign-in hung on my dead domain, so I moved it.

Talli's Stripe webhook had rejected purchases since Oct 2, and that is fixed now.

My portfolio moved to jaybulb.com; App Store apps keep the old address until next build.

Wordroot's homepage is now the app, in Apple style, and I dropped its custom font because it looked like Pokemon.

## Oct 17

Tonchi is Patois, Hikko is Thinkpond and Windgate is Airwell, with repos and listings renamed. All 41 apps got a brand kit and a new icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I patched about ten repos. Patois now teaches before it quizzes, live for Spanish units 1 to 5. Apple's rejections came from old builds calling my lapsed domain, so I swapped hosts. Thinkpond is back in App Review, and Mailbag, Epiphany and Intake need new builds to ship.

The limit on Unreal is RAM, not skill. The editor took 16 minutes to boot from the LaCie, then ran out of memory. Unreal gets one day a week until a 1 TB SSD lands and the LaCie goes back to Dad.

## Oct 18

Cruise had slowed from about 16k XP a day to 11k. Its balance mode kept picking the course furthest behind, so the runner sat on slow courses. Now a course has to be fast enough to get picked.

Atlin got a folded-map-and-pin icon, which I gave an A+. The build in review adds Sign in with Apple, account deletion and saves that follow you between devices. Since then I added price drops, a Size filter, daily saved-search alerts, draw-on-map search, walk scores, price history, open houses and matching sign-in buttons. Zillow and REW.ca wait for that build, since the scraping service hit its monthly cap and I won't pay more.

Turing can cut, join, mute and speed up videos, turn a clip into a GIF, and pull or trim its sound. She also works on 3D files through Blender. I made her a garage glyph for a garage door company's logo and moved her to turing.jaybulb.com. A bigger base model didn't beat her picker, so that stays.

Joshua Tree had been red, because its checks fetched Samantha's face from the lapsed heyitsmejosh.com. The domain move and a caption-aware check are in CI, and the windows rework is done: four apps open at once, and a crash in one spares the rest.

Mailbag and Patois went into App Review. Blackcomb, my browser, got a Safari-style start page, a blue mountain icon and a front page with snow falling over a mountain. I built a way for an AI to drive it on the Mac, switched off and held back until Apple is done with that version. I answered Apple's questions this afternoon, and the Mac version went straight back into review.

Nimble had its best day yet. Everyday math like half of a number or a tip works offline, "Tell me more" works on every platform, and stock prices came back after the old quote feed locked me out. The landing and web app got an Apple pass. My X thread is up, and Reddit's spam filter hid my post until the mods approve it. Nim, the mascot, went onto Android and desktop, and the app speaks nine languages on every platform.

I filed nine more Apple Notes into the roadmaps and wiki, and left one secret in Notes on purpose. Four native apps still point at Supabase and need new builds.

I folded GitHub from 62 repos to 31. Twenty-five small projects now sit as folders in one repo, labs, with their history, and four private ones went into a repo called vault.

The Mac mini filled its disk in an update, and I cleared caches by hand. Free space is 9GB, and I want to keep it there.
