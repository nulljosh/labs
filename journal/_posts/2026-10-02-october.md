## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree reached 2.0 with twenty-six apps in protected boxes. Bookrank is live on iPhone with natural voices and offline reading.

Premium is $1 and auto-trading is live. My paper-traded Double 7s rule hit 77 percent. Notate is live on iPhone.

## Oct 4

A Pi 4B is coming for hardware tests.

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review.

Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign; iOS is live.


## Oct 5

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a refreshed logo on Samantha's face.

Apple rejected seven apps as spam and Hikko over screenshots.


NYC Survive's overnight loop made it winnable; iOS is live, macOS in review.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a cache flush fix.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi after I wrote the login handshake its chip lacked.

Samantha answers on the real Pi on my API credit. My Mac relay picks a cheap or strong model per question, with read-only access to one folder. Money comes from a Pi dev kit; the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote Joshua Tree's eleven-chapter book with board photos.

After a DNS fix it loaded heyitsmejosh.com over HTTPS; scripts still don't run. Every Pi image carried my relay key; the fix is in review.

## Oct 9 to 10

I halved my custom skills and flashed the Pi card.

## Oct 11

I built xread, a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models. Most web apps moved off Supabase, so everyone resets passwords. Heyitsmejosh.com lapses on the 13th; the brand moves to jaybulb.com.

Turing can drive Joshua Tree from chat on her build machines only.


Four repos took new names and eleven App Store listings moved to jaybulb.com. 
Twenty Joshua Tree changes merged, and my first Pi 4 run boots to a desktop. Bluetooth finds my mouse but hangs up before pairing.

## Oct 12

Turing's picker clears all three dev bars, and its C tool fires on only 20 of 434 no-tool questions. The sealed sets stay unread.

I folded ten Pi pull requests into one and flashed the card. It boots, and the mouse reconnects by itself. Multi-window is broken, the boot bar is choppy and the wallpaper is terracotta.

## Oct 13

I cut Joshua Tree's docs from fifty-six files to eleven. I approved a plan to make the OS a daily driver. On the real card my mouse survives reboot, but dragging redraws the whole screen. Turing's picker made 115 false calls against a bar of 22; an abstain step fixes that, PR open.


## Oct 14

I built Noema on jaybulb.com. First-descent checks passed past a million, and Lean checked one small proof.


## Oct 15

I synced sixty skills into Codex and merged fifty-seven Apple Notes into twenty-three.

## Oct 16

I filed 33 of 34 Apple Notes into roadmaps, then lost a day to updates.

jaybulb.com had no mail records, so nothing sent there could arrive. Now every address reaches my inbox.

Reset emails now carry each app's name. Quotestreak got a forgot-password page.

Hikko's sign-in hung on my dead domain, so I moved it.

Talli's Stripe webhook rejected purchases since Oct 2, and that is fixed now.

My portfolio moved to jaybulb.com; App Store apps keep the old address until next build.

Wordroot's homepage is now the app, in Apple style, and I dropped its font for looking like Pokemon.

## Oct 17

Tonchi is Patois, Hikko is Thinkpond and Windgate is Airwell. All 41 apps got a brand kit and a new icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I patched about ten repos. Patois teaches before it quizzes, live for Spanish 1 to 5. Apple's rejections came from old builds calling my lapsed domain, so I swapped hosts. Thinkpond is back in App Review; Mailbag, Epiphany and Intake need new builds.

The limit on Unreal is RAM, not skill. The editor took 16 minutes to boot from the LaCie, then ran out of memory. Unreal gets one day a week until a 1 TB SSD lands and the LaCie goes back to Dad.

## Oct 18

Cruise slowed from about 16k XP a day to 11k. Its balance mode kept picking the course furthest behind, so it sat on slow ones. Now a course has to be fast enough to get picked.

Atlin got a folded-map-and-pin icon I gave an A+. The build in review adds Sign in with Apple, account deletion and saves that follow you between devices. Since then I added price drops, a Size filter, saved-search alerts, draw-on-map search, walk scores, price history, open houses and matching sign-in buttons. Zillow and REW.ca wait for that build; the scraper hit its monthly cap and I won't pay more.

Turing can cut, join, mute and speed up videos, make GIFs, and pull or trim sound. She also works on 3D files through Blender. I made her a garage glyph for a garage door company's logo and moved her to turing.jaybulb.com. A bigger base model didn't beat her picker, so that stays.

Joshua Tree was red because its checks fetched Samantha's face from my lapsed domain. Moving off that domain fixed CI, and the windows rework is done: four apps open at once, and a crash spares the rest.

Mailbag and Patois went into App Review. Blackcomb, my browser, got a Safari-style start page, a blue mountain icon and a snowy front page. I built an AI driver for it on the Mac, held back until Apple finishes with that version. I answered Apple's questions this afternoon; the Mac version went straight back into review.

Nimble had its best day yet. Everyday math like half of a number or a tip works offline, "Tell me more" works on every platform, and stock prices came back after the old quote feed locked me out. The landing and web app got an Apple pass. My X thread is up, and Reddit's spam filter holds my post for the mods. Nim went onto Android and desktop; the app speaks nine languages everywhere.

Tonight I ran my X reader again. It pulled 37 bookmarks and 72 tweets into a digest, then I cleared them off X. Two ideas came out of it: a check that stops any file too big for an AI to read at once, and a /show-me skill that records a flow as a video and sends it to me.

I filed nine more Apple Notes into the roadmaps and wiki, and left one secret in Notes on purpose. Four native apps still point at Supabase and need new builds.

I folded GitHub from 62 repos to 31. Twenty-five small projects are folders in one repo, labs, history kept, and four private ones went into vault.

An update filled the Mac mini's disk, so I cleared caches by hand. Free space is 9GB, and I want it kept that way.
