## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree reached 2.0 with twenty-six apps in protected boxes. Bookrank is live on iPhone with natural voices and offline reading.

Premium is $1 and auto-trading is live. My paper-traded Double 7s rule hit 77 percent. Notate is live on iPhone.

## Oct 4

A Pi 4B is coming for hardware tests.

My idea app became Hikko, since Hotaru was taken; iPhone and Mac are in review.

Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign; iOS is live.


## Oct 5

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a refreshed logo on Samantha's face.

Apple rejected seven apps as spam and Hikko over screenshots.


NYC Survive's overnight loop made it winnable; iOS is live, macOS in review.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a cache flush fix.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi after I wrote the login handshake its chip lacked.

Samantha answers on the real Pi on my API credit. My Mac relay picks a cheap or strong model per question and gives her read-only access to one folder. An overnight runner works branches only, $5 cap per run. Money comes from a Pi dev kit; the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote Joshua Tree's eleven-chapter book with board photos.

After a DNS fix it loaded heyitsmejosh.com over HTTPS; scripts still don't run. Every Pi image carried my relay key; the fix is in review.

## Oct 9 to 10

I halved my custom skills and flashed the Pi card.

## Oct 11

I built xread, a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models. Most web apps moved off Supabase, so everyone resets passwords. Heyitsmejosh.com lapses on the 13th; the brand moves to jaybulb.com.

Turing can drive Joshua Tree from chat on her build machines only.


Four repos took new names and eleven App Store listings moved to jaybulb.com. 
Twenty Joshua Tree changes merged, and my first Pi 4 run boots to a desktop. Bluetooth finds my mouse but hangs up before pairing.

## Oct 12

Turing's picker clears all three dev bars, and its C tool fires on only 20 of 434 no-tool questions. The sealed sets stay unread.

I folded ten Pi pull requests into one and flashed the card. It boots, and the mouse reconnects by itself. Multi-window is broken, the boot bar is choppy and the wallpaper is terracotta.

## Oct 13

I cut Joshua Tree's docs from fifty-six files to eleven. I approved a plan to make the OS a daily driver. On the real card my mouse survives reboot, but dragging redraws the whole screen. Turing's picker made 115 false calls against a bar of 22; an abstain step fixes that, PR open.


## Oct 14

I built Noema on jaybulb.com. First-descent checks passed past a million, and Lean checked one small proof.


## Oct 15

I synced sixty skills into Codex and merged fifty-seven Apple Notes into twenty-three.

## Oct 16

I filed 33 of 34 Apple Notes into roadmaps, then lost a day to updates.

jaybulb.com had no mail records, so nothing sent there could arrive. Now every address reaches my inbox.

Reset emails now carry each app's name. Quotestreak got a forgot-password page, and Brick's link has a new domain.

Hikko's sign-in hung on my dead domain, so I moved it.

Talli's pages went from cream to white. Its Stripe webhook had rejected purchases since Oct 2, fixed with a new signing secret. A locked BC Self-Serve account, not the code, caused its Invalid credentials error.

My portfolio moved to jaybulb.com; App Store apps keep the old address until next build.

Wordroot's homepage is now the app, in Apple style, and I dropped its custom font because it looked like Pokemon.

## Oct 17

Tonchi is Patois, Hikko is Thinkpond and Windgate is Airwell, with repos and listings renamed. All 41 apps got a brand kit and a new icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I patched about ten repos and sent favicons to about thirty sites. Patois now teaches before it quizzes, live for Spanish units 1 to 5. Apple's rejections came from old builds calling my lapsed domain, so I swapped hosts. Thinkpond is back in App Review with a new build and demo account, and Mailbag, Epiphany and Intake need new builds to ship.

The limit on Unreal is RAM, not skill. The editor took 16 minutes to boot from the LaCie and then ran out of memory. Unreal gets one day a week until a 1 TB SSD lands and the LaCie goes back to Dad; renting a GPU by the hour is worth pricing first. Until then the work stays on the web.

## Oct 18

Cruise had slowed from about 16k XP a day to 11k, and I found why: its balance mode kept picking the course furthest behind, so the runner sat on slow courses like Japanese and chess. Now a course has to be fast enough to get picked. Cruise is at 602,884 XP with a 193-day streak.

Atlin got a folded-map-and-pin icon, which I gave an A+, and blue sign-up emails. The build in review adds Sign in with Apple, account deletion and saves that follow you between devices. Since then I added price drops, a Size filter, daily alerts for saved searches and all 27 languages. It all waits behind that build.

Turing can now cut, join, mute and speed up videos, turn a clip into a GIF, and pull or trim its sound. She also works on 3D files through Blender, and she notes every request she can't handle. I made her a garage glyph for a garage door company's logo and moved her to turing.jaybulb.com. I tried a bigger base model for her picker, and it didn't beat the one she ships with, so that stays. A bigger one is training now.

Joshua Tree had been red for a while, because its checks fetched Samantha's face from heyitsmejosh.com, which lapsed. The domain move and a caption-aware check are in CI now. The windows rework is done too: four apps open at once, and a crash in one spares the rest.

Mailbag went into App Review on iPhone and Mac, and Blackcomb got a Safari-style start page with a blue mountain icon.

Nimble now asks Groq's free models first, so the free AI lasts the day. Nim, the mascot, went onto Android and desktop, and the app speaks nine languages on every platform, with an Apple Watch app and lock screen widgets. The store submissions are mine to do next.

The Mac mini filled its disk during an update and the shell stopped working until I cleared caches by hand. Free space is back to about 14GB, and I want to keep it there.
