## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree hit 2.0 with twenty-six apps in protected boxes; Bookrank is live offline on iPhone.

Premium is $1, auto-trading is live, and my paper rule hit 77 percent.

## Oct 4

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review.

Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign; iOS is live.

## Oct 5

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a logo on Samantha's face.

Apple rejected seven apps as spam and Hikko over screenshots.

NYC Survive's overnight loop made it winnable; iOS is live, macOS in review.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a cache fix.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi after I wrote the login handshake its chip lacked.

Samantha answers on the real Pi. Money comes from a Pi dev kit; the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote Joshua Tree's eleven-chapter book with board photos. I halved my custom skills and flashed the Pi card.

After a DNS fix it loaded heyitsmejosh.com over HTTPS; scripts don't run.

## Oct 11

I built xread, a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models. Most web apps left Supabase, so everyone resets passwords.

## Oct 12

Turing's picker clears all three dev bars; its C tool fires on 20 of 434 no-tool questions.

## Oct 13

I cut Joshua Tree's docs from fifty-six files to eleven and approved a plan to make the OS a daily driver. On the real card my mouse survives reboot, but dragging redraws everything. Turing's picker made 115 false calls; an abstain step is next.

## Oct 14

I built Noema on jaybulb.com; Lean checked a small proof.

## Oct 16

jaybulb.com had no mail records, so nothing sent there arrived; now every address works.

Reset emails carry each app's name. Talli's Stripe webhook rejected purchases since Oct 2; fixed.

My portfolio moved to jaybulb.com; App Store apps keep the old one until the next build.

## Oct 17

Tonchi is Patois, Hikko is Thinkpond and Windgate is Airwell. All 41 apps got a new brand kit and icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I fixed ten repos. Apple's rejections came from my lapsed domain, so I swapped hosts.

Unreal's limit is RAM, so it gets one day a week until a 1 TB SSD lands and the LaCie goes back to Dad.

## Oct 18

Cruise fell from 16k XP a day to 11k on slow courses, so it picks only fast ones.

Atlin got an A+ icon, price drops, alerts, map search, walk scores and open houses.

Joshua Tree was red because its checks hit my old domain. Windows rework is done: four apps open at once, and a crash spares the rest. The Pi got admin accounts and a login screen.

Mailbag and Patois went into App Review, and Quotestreak got a Daily Quote. Dave, my dad's friend, bought Blockprint.

"Tell me more" works everywhere; stock prices came back after the old quote feed locked me out. Nim went onto Android and desktop in nine languages.

I cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31 and erased my plan folder from public history; only my vault has it.

## Oct 19

Quotestreak, now Coda, got a whitepaper, 14,000 Wikiquote lines.

Notate's big update went live on iPhone and Apple Watch, offline.

Nimble's offline pack grew to 14,000 Wikipedia articles, and Pre-Calc 12 works offline.

Glyphbox, now Icelus, became a design tool with layers, masks, auto layout, and a chat that draws a screen from one sentence. The web is live; the App Store build waits.

My litigation app is Temida now, in six languages, with a public repo after I scrubbed case details. The iPhone app bounced over screenshots, so I redid them with a made-up case. Both are back in review.

Thinkpond went back to review, but it kind of sucked, so it's Melete.

My jaybulb.com portfolio is one list of fifty projects, ranked by how far each shipped. I dropped 33 of 95 dead links.

I added Hermes 3 beside Qwen, the default. Qwen wrote better code; Hermes was faster in chat but botched git rebase.

On the Pi, holding Space talks to Samantha, who opens apps and fetches weather. I split Joshua Tree's biggest source file and cut its test run from 46 minutes to 15.

I built Hermes, a Helm clone for App Store Connect. Its iPhone app answers reviews once I confirm each, and Resolve All drafts replies and GitHub issues for each rejection, using a free local model. Nothing sends unchecked.

## Oct 20

Mailbag got a new landing and blue icons, but the store build is pink. It cleared my mail, iCloud and Gmail to zero.

Nimble passes every BC grade 10 to 12 math, science and socials exam offline at A+, and Calculus I passes 120 of 120. New versions score low on first runs; I'd rather show that. Confident wrong word-problem answers fell from 46 to 2, and the first web load is 80 KB lighter.

Talli's QA pass caught a wrong cheque date: it said October 28, but BC issues it on the 21st. The tables match the government's schedules. Settings and Sign Out were unreachable on iPhone; fixed. The iPhone app got a Liquid Glass pass and the Mac version a sidebar, and sign-in screens now link to a benefit finder that needs no account. The Watch app works without the phone, pending a real watch check. The iPhone update and the Mac app are both in Apple's review.

I fixed Talli's Alberta earnings rule to the official $350 a month, and the apps keep your last numbers for when the signal drops.

Epiphany got a blue bulb icon, its own font and bouncy pins, and I closed all 45 sweep problems. Then an Apollo theme with laurel-leaf quotes; I kept the name. It's in Apple review on iPhone and Mac.

The calling app went through Liana, Marmoset, Irisa and Oohah, which another Apple account owned, so it's Oohaah. It has a block list and an 18-plus gate. Both builds are in App Review, and calls work on cellular.

I renamed Notate back to Echo and gave it a Jony Ive pass. My benchmark had counted an audiobook's credit line as errors; fixed, Apple's speech misses 1.5 percent of words on a human voice. The big one: a keyboard that dictates into any app. Keyboards can't use the mic, so it opens Echo to record in the background. Echo also dropped its base model, saving 143 MB. I haven't tried the keyboard on a real iPhone yet. The store still says Notate.

Cruise, my Duolingo autoplayer, sat dead from 1:20 to 10:55, probably after a cleanup. The streak held at 195 since the day's goal was met; XP is near 670k.
Carton, a new UPS tracker with a delivery countdown, has its first code now.
