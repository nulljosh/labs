## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree hit 2.0 with twenty-six apps in protected boxes; Bookrank works offline on iPhone.

Premium is $1, auto-trading is live, and my paper rule hit 77%.

## Oct 4

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review.

Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign; iOS is live.

## Oct 5

Apple rejected seven apps as spam and Hikko over screenshots.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a cache fix.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi after I wrote the login handshake its chip lacked. Money comes from a Pi dev kit; the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote Joshua Tree's eleven-chapter book with board photos and flashed the Pi card.

## Oct 11

I built a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models.

## Oct 13

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a logo on Samantha's face. I cut its docs from fifty-six files to eleven. Turing's picker made 115 false calls; an abstain step is next.

## Oct 16

jaybulb.com had no mail records, so nothing arrived; now every address works.

Talli's Stripe webhook rejected purchases since Oct 2; fixed.

## Oct 17

All 41 apps got a new brand kit and icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I fixed ten repos. Apple's rejections came from my lapsed domain, so I swapped hosts.

## Oct 18

Cruise fell from 16k XP a day to 11k on slow courses, so it picks fast ones.

Windows rework is done: four apps open at once, and a crash spares the rest.

Mailbag and Patois went into App Review, Quotestreak got a Daily Quote, and stock prices came back.

I cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31 and erased my plan folder from public history.

## Oct 19

Quotestreak, now Coda, got a whitepaper and 14,000 Wikiquote lines. Notate's big update went live on iPhone and Watch, offline. Glyphbox became Icelus; the App Store build waits.

My litigation app is Temida now, with a public repo after I scrubbed case details. Thinkpond went back to review, but it sucked, so it's Melete.

My portfolio is one ranked list of fifty projects, and I dropped 33 of 95 dead links.

On the Pi, holding Space talks to Samantha, who opens apps and fetches weather.

I built Hermes, a Helm clone for App Store Connect that sends nothing without my yes.

## Oct 20

Nimble passes every BC grade 10 to 12 math, science and socials exam offline at A+. Confident wrong word-problem answers fell from 46 to 2.

Talli's QA caught a wrong cheque date and a dead Sign Out on iPhone; both are fixed. Its Alberta earnings rule uses the official $350 a month.

Epiphany got a blue bulb icon and all 45 sweep problems closed.

The calling app went through Liana, Marmoset, Irisa and Oohah, which another account owned, so it's Oohaah. Both builds are in review.

I renamed Notate to Echo; the store still says Notate.

Cartons went into App Review on iPhone and Mac for 99 cents.

## Oct 21

Patois uses its new name everywhere, and its streak-freeze crash is fixed. Temida's Sign in with Apple fix cleared on iPhone and Mac.

Intake was taken on the App Store, so it's Machaon. Its native apps moved from Supabase to Cloudflare, so web and phone share one account.

Epiphany's Mac build is in review. The web and phone disagreed about my money, so I fixed my income, wiped old debt, and scrubbed my money from its public history.

Curvely 2.0 went into App Review on iPhone and Mac. Claude named it Tangent, then Pan; the store name waits.

NYC Survive is Gastown now: a crew, a Steam Clock and self-running drills, with no raids, weapons or cash. A headless bot called it unwinnable; now both win in twelve days.

I rebuilt the BC Garage Doors site to match bcgaragedoors.ca, minus its made-up prices and claims. Doorstock, with parts, jobs and reorder emails, went to Apple for Mac with a Today screen.

## Oct 22

Epiphany splits subscriptions by currency, so a US$200 plan stops landing in my Canadian totals. Its apps read Fear & Greed from CNN, since my server is blocked.

Apple rejected the first Cartons and Oohaah builds; reasons pending.

Doorstock syncs shops, crews and jobs, and garage door leads left Supabase.

My Duolingo runner is Surf now and open source. A disk cleanup killed it from Wednesday; I moved it out of cleanup's reach and saved the 197-day streak.

I filled the missing units of my Pre-Calc 12 masterclass.

Unreal Engine kept reopening at login after three failed fixes. The culprit was a leftover Epic launcher agent. It's off and locked, but I won't trust it until reboot.

## Oct 25

Joshua Tree's main source file shrank by over half, and Samantha now answers on the Pi without my Mac.

I renamed Coranto to Bugle. Apple refused plain Bugle, so the store name is BugleNews, and both submissions were pulled. Accounts sync feeds now.

Each outlet wears its own color, and the app takes that color when only one shows. Epiphany's news now comes from Yahoo Finance and CNBC, so it's never empty.

Clock, a one-page clock with alarms and timers, got a blue brand and a landing page running the real clock.

I took Hermes from 4.0 to 10.0 that afternoon: a grid of every app, an iPad split view, release timelines, Xcode Cloud runs and a keyword audit.

Five Apple appeals were confirmed: Cartons, Sidewise, Oohaah, Atlin and Talli. Atlin's store name was stuck on a leftover test name, so I put it back.

Melete's Mac build lacked the Sign in with Apple permission, and Machaon's Mac app stopped on a keychain prompt the reviewer couldn't answer. Both are fixed and resubmitted; Machaon lost the Healstack name.

Oohaah got a private repo, and Talli's privacy page now names Cloudflare.

## Oct 26

I signed my Ja Gmail into Mailbag and ran Clear inbox twice until it sat at zero. It filed 14 mails into folders, archived 4, and deleted nothing.

Echo's keyboard is with Apple for review on iPhone, untested on a real phone. Custom words now fix the transcript too.

Episodes open with their cover as a wide hero that blurs under the bar as you scroll, and Hacker News stories open in the app with their comments. Swiping left saves in green. I rebuilt Home the way I'd want Apple to build it: a big title with the date, a round glass Briefing button, and search as its own tab. Then came Ground News style collections, a shelf of your podcast shows, unread dots and a compact layout.

The Duolingo runner's browser is muted now, since lessons were reading aloud on my Mac.
