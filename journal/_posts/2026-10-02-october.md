## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree hit 2.0 with twenty-six apps in protected boxes, and Bookrank is live on iPhone with natural voices and offline reading.

Premium is $1, auto-trading is live, and my paper-traded Double 7s rule hit 77 percent. Notate is live on iPhone.

## Oct 4

A Pi 4B is coming for hardware tests.

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review.

Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign; iOS is live.

## Oct 5

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a refreshed logo on Samantha's face.

Apple rejected seven apps as spam and Hikko over screenshots.

NYC Survive's overnight loop made it winnable; iOS is live, macOS in review.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a cache flush fix.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi after I wrote the login handshake its chip lacked.

Samantha answers on the real Pi, and my Mac relay picks a cheap or strong model per question. Money comes from a Pi dev kit; the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote Joshua Tree's eleven-chapter book with board photos.

After a DNS fix it loaded heyitsmejosh.com over HTTPS; scripts still don't run. Every Pi image carried my relay key; the fix is in review.

## Oct 9 to 10

I halved my custom skills and flashed the Pi card.

## Oct 11

I built xread, a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models. Most web apps moved off Supabase, so everyone resets passwords. Heyitsmejosh.com lapses on the 13th; the brand moves to jaybulb.com.

Turing can drive Joshua Tree from chat on her build machines only.

Four repos took new names, eleven App Store listings moved to jaybulb.com, twenty Joshua Tree changes merged, and my first Pi 4 run booted to a desktop.

## Oct 12

Turing's picker clears all three dev bars, and its C tool fires on only 20 of 434 no-tool questions; the sealed sets stay unread.

I folded ten Pi pull requests into one and flashed the card. It boots and the mouse reconnects, but multi-window is broken, the boot bar is choppy and the wallpaper is terracotta.

## Oct 13

I cut Joshua Tree's docs from fifty-six files to eleven and approved a plan to make the OS a daily driver. On the real card my mouse survives reboot, but dragging redraws the whole screen. Turing's picker made 115 false calls against a bar of 22; an abstain step fixes that, PR open.

## Oct 14

I built Noema on jaybulb.com; its checks passed past a million, and Lean checked one small proof.

## Oct 15

I merged fifty-seven Apple Notes into twenty-three.

## Oct 16

I filed 33 of 34 Apple Notes into roadmaps, then lost a day to updates.

jaybulb.com had no mail records, so nothing sent there could arrive; now every address reaches my inbox.

Reset emails carry each app's name, and Quotestreak got a forgot-password page. Hikko's sign-in hung on my dead domain, so I moved it. Talli's Stripe webhook had rejected purchases since Oct 2, now fixed.

My portfolio moved to jaybulb.com, and App Store apps keep the old address until the next build. Wordroot's homepage is now the app in Apple style, and I dropped its font for looking like Pokemon.

## Oct 17

Tonchi is Patois, Hikko is Thinkpond and Windgate is Airwell. All 41 apps got a brand kit and a new icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I patched about ten repos. Apple's rejections came from old builds calling my lapsed domain, so I swapped hosts. Thinkpond is back in App Review; Mailbag, Epiphany and Intake need new builds.

The limit on Unreal is RAM, not skill: the editor took 16 minutes to boot from the LaCie, then ran out of memory. Unreal gets one day a week until a 1 TB SSD lands and the LaCie goes back to Dad.

## Oct 18

Cruise slowed from about 16k XP a day to 11k. Its balance mode kept picking the course furthest behind, so it sat on slow ones. Now a course has to be fast enough to earn a pick.

Atlin got a folded-map-and-pin icon I gave an A+. The build in review adds Sign in with Apple, account deletion and saves that follow you between devices. Since then I've added price drops, alerts, draw-on-map search, walk scores, price history and open houses. Zillow and REW.ca are on, so the web shows real rentals and sales; US sale is Zillow and Craigslist only, because the other feeds still block me.

Turing can cut, join, mute and speed up videos, make GIFs, pull and trim sound, and make 3D files through Blender. I gave her a garage glyph for a garage door company's logo and moved her to turing.jaybulb.com. A bigger base model didn't beat her picker, so that stays.

Joshua Tree was red because its checks fetched Samantha's face from my lapsed domain; moving off it fixed CI. The windows rework is done: four apps open at once, and a crash spares the rest. On the Pi I added accounts with admin rights and a login screen.

Mailbag and Patois went into App Review, and Quotestreak got a Daily Quote and went back to Apple. Blockprint got a block icon, a landing page and a third version that matches across every device. Blackcomb, my browser, got a Safari-style start page and a mountain icon. Its Mac AI driver waits for Apple.

Nimble had its best day yet. Everyday math like half a number or a tip works offline, and so do physical constants. "Tell me more" works on every platform, and stock prices came back after the old quote feed locked me out. Nim went onto Android and desktop in nine languages.

My X reader pulled 37 bookmarks into a digest tonight. Two ideas came out of it: a size check for files too big for an AI to read at once, and a show-me tool that records a flow as a video.

I filed nine more Apple Notes into roadmaps and the wiki. I also folded GitHub from 62 repos to 31: twenty-five small projects are folders in one repo, and four private ones went into vault. An update filled the Mac mini's disk; I cleared caches by hand and want nine gigabytes kept free.

I audited Tripwire, the watcher that files an issue when a repo's CI goes red. Detection works, but the fixer it was meant to have had never opened a pull request. It now files one issue per failing workflow instead of per run, closes it on green, and ignores cancelled runs. I closed fourteen duplicates an uptime cron had piled up in labs. A forced run found one real failure, and the fixer skipped it because the job log came back empty. The real fixer is still to build.
