## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree hit 2.0 with twenty-six apps in protected boxes; Bookrank works offline on iPhone.

Premium is $1, auto-trading is live, and my paper rule hit 77%.

## Oct 4

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review.

Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign; iOS is live.

## Oct 5

Apple rejected seven apps as spam and Hikko over screenshots.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a cache fix.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi after I wrote the login handshake its chip lacked. Money comes from a Pi dev kit; the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote Joshua Tree's eleven-chapter book with board photos and flashed the Pi card.

## Oct 11

I built a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models.

## Oct 13

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a logo on Samantha's face.## Oct 16

jaybulb.com had no mail records, so nothing arrived; now every address works.

## Oct 17

All 41 apps got a new brand kit and icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I fixed ten repos. Apple's rejections came from my lapsed domain, so I swapped hosts.

## Oct 18

Cruise fell from 16k XP a day to 11k on slow courses, so it picks fast ones.

Mailbag and Patois went into App Review, Quotestreak got a Daily Quote, and stock prices came back.

I cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31 and erased my plan folder from public history.

## Oct 19

Quotestreak, now Coda, got a whitepaper. Notate's big update went live on iPhone and Watch, offline. Glyphbox became Icelus; the App Store build waits.

My litigation app is Temida now, with a public repo after I scrubbed case details. Thinkpond became Melete.

My portfolio is one ranked list of fifty projects, and I dropped 33 of 95 dead links.

## Oct 20

Nimble passes every BC grade 10 to 12 math, science and socials exam offline at A+. Confident wrong word-problem answers fell from 46 to 2.

Talli's QA caught a wrong cheque date and a dead Sign Out on iPhone; both are fixed.
The calling app went through three names before Oohaah, which another account owned. Both builds are in review.

I renamed Notate to Echo; the store still says Notate.

Cartons went into App Review on iPhone and Mac for 99 cents.

## Oct 21

Patois uses its new name everywhere, and its streak-freeze crash is fixed. Temida's Sign in with Apple fix cleared on iPhone and Mac.

Intake was taken on the App Store, so it's Machaon. Its native apps moved from Supabase to Cloudflare, so web and phone share one account.

Epiphany's Mac build is in review. The web and phone disagreed about my money, so I fixed my income, wiped old debt and scrubbed my money from its public history.

Curvely 2.0 went into App Review on iPhone and Mac. Claude named it Tangent, then Pan; the store name waits.

NYC Survive is Gastown now: a crew, a Steam Clock and self-running drills, no raids or cash. A headless bot called it unwinnable; now both win in twelve days.

I rebuilt the BC Garage Doors site to match bcgaragedoors.ca, minus its made-up prices and claims. Doorstock, with parts, jobs and reorder emails, went to Apple for Mac with a Today screen.

## Oct 22

Epiphany splits subscriptions by currency, so a US$200 plan stops landing in my Canadian totals.
My Duolingo runner is Surf now and open source. A disk cleanup killed it from Wednesday; I moved it out of cleanup's reach and saved the 197-day streak.

Unreal kept reopening at login; the culprit was a leftover Epic launcher agent. It's off and locked, but I won't trust it until reboot.

## Oct 25

Joshua Tree's main source file shrank by over half, and Samantha now answers on the Pi without my Mac.

I renamed Coranto to Bugle. Apple refused plain Bugle, so the store name is BugleNews, and both submissions were pulled.

Each outlet wears its own color, and the app uses it when only one shows. Epiphany's news now comes from Yahoo Finance and CNBC, so it's never empty.

Clock became Horula, since every clock name I tried was taken on the App Store. By night it had twelve languages, a world map with a day and night line, an analog stopwatch and an embeddable clock.

I took Hermes from 4.0 to 10.0 that afternoon: a grid of every app, an iPad split view, release timelines, Xcode Cloud runs and a keyword audit.

Five Apple appeals were confirmed: Cartons, Sidewise, Oohaah, Atlin and Talli. Atlin's store name was stuck on a leftover test name, so I put it back.

Melete's Mac build lacked the Sign in with Apple permission, and Machaon's Mac app stopped on a keychain prompt the reviewer couldn't answer. Both are fixed and resubmitted; Machaon lost the Healstack name.

## Oct 26

I ran Mailbag's Clear inbox on my Ja Gmail until it sat at zero, filing 14 mails and archiving 4 without deleting any.

Echo's keyboard is with Apple for review on iPhone and Mac, untested on a real phone. Custom words now fix the transcript too.

Episodes open on a blurred cover hero, and Hacker News stories open with their comments. Home got rebuilt the way Apple would build it: a big date title, a round glass Briefing button and search as its own tab. Then came Ground News style collections, a podcast shelf, unread dots, a mascot icon, a boot splash and a first-run topic picker.

Horula finished every item on its rivals' list, from city nicknames and countdowns to a flip face, spoken interval cues, Wake Up Check, routines, sleep sounds and Solve to Stop. Alarms can't be blocked on iPhone, so Wake Up Check and Solve to Stop ring again three minutes later; the Watch can turn that off. Rain joined the sleep sounds, and the apps wait on Apple review.

Surf, my Duolingo runner, also plays FEZ alone and has 24 of its 32 cube bits. Its browser is muted; lessons were reading aloud on my Mac.

Patois's language packs copy the vocabulary Surf already solved, so Turkish went from 11 units to 120. I booked eight Product Hunt launches for Oct 28 to Nov 6, and Joshua Tree can back up my home folder to the Mac.

Joshua Tree 6.0 is in CI as a pull request: a sixth tuft on the logo, each person's own wallpaper, and a three-minute cap on each live Samantha check. It merges on green, which clears my goal of no open issues or pull requests. The SD card for my board pass is flashed with it, and the Nimbus case has a parts list. The launch film took ten versions; the first got a C+.
