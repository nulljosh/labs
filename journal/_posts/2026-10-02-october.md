## Oct 27

Curbfind is Scrounge now, live at scrounge.jaybulb.com; the store still says Curbfind until Apple login works.

Nimble got Raycast and Alfred extensions. Mailbag's Mac build reads Mail.app now, so Telus and other providers work. Airwell's iPhone build was rejected, and I can't read why.

Patois passed dark mode and phone-width checks, with 128 courses. Icelus got transitions on the web and in Swift; its npm and registry launches wait on me.

Noema had its biggest day yet. I finished a full Lean proof that Busy Beaver 2,3 is 38, then a C program counted every halting five-state Turing machine. Its count matches the published one, and the halting times add up to what I think is a new OEIS term. The same run found 1,207 distinct runtimes, which looks like an answer to a question Scott Aaronson asked in 2020. These are candidates, not breakthroughs, and two OEIS drafts wait for me to submit them. In medicine, I checked ClinicalTrials.gov baseline numbers against their arms: 407 standard deviations no arm size could produce, and 61 confidence intervals in 52 trials with a minus sign lost or added. Their own p-values back the sign, and two linked papers print it right.
