## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree hit 2.0 with twenty-six apps in protected boxes, and Bookrank is live on iPhone with offline reading.

Premium is $1, auto-trading is live, and my paper-traded Double 7s rule hit 77 percent.

## Oct 4

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review.

Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign; iOS is live.

## Oct 5

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a logo on Samantha's face.

Apple rejected seven apps as spam and Hikko over screenshots.

NYC Survive's overnight loop made it winnable; iOS is live, macOS in review.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a cache flush fix.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi after I wrote the login handshake its chip lacked.

Samantha answers on the real Pi; my Mac relay picks a cheap or strong model per question. Money comes from a Pi dev kit; the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote Joshua Tree's eleven-chapter book with board photos.

After a DNS fix it loaded heyitsmejosh.com over HTTPS; scripts don't run. Every Pi image carried my relay key; the fix is in review.

## Oct 9 to 10

I halved my custom skills and flashed the Pi card.

## Oct 11

I built xread, a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models. Most web apps moved off Supabase, so everyone resets passwords. Heyitsmejosh.com lapses on the 13th.

Four repos took new names, eleven App Store listings moved to jaybulb.com, twenty Joshua Tree changes merged, and my first Pi 4 run booted to a desktop.

## Oct 12

Turing's picker clears all three dev bars, and its C tool fires on only 20 of 434 no-tool questions.

I folded ten Pi pull requests into one and flashed the card. It boots and the mouse reconnects, but multi-window is broken and the boot bar stutters.

## Oct 13

I cut Joshua Tree's docs from fifty-six files to eleven and approved a plan to make the OS a daily driver. On the real card my mouse survives reboot, but dragging redraws the whole screen. Turing's picker made 115 false calls; an abstain step fixes that, PR open.

## Oct 14

I built Noema on jaybulb.com; Lean checked one small proof.

## Oct 15

I merged fifty-seven Apple Notes into twenty-three.

## Oct 16

jaybulb.com had no mail records, so nothing sent there could arrive; now every address reaches me.

Reset emails carry each app's name, and Quotestreak got a forgot-password page. Hikko's sign-in hung on my dead domain, so I moved it. Talli's Stripe webhook had rejected purchases since Oct 2, now fixed.

My portfolio moved to jaybulb.com; App Store apps keep the old address until their next build.

## Oct 17

Tonchi is Patois, Hikko is Thinkpond and Windgate is Airwell. All 41 apps got a new brand kit and icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I patched about ten repos. Apple's rejections came from builds calling my lapsed domain, so I swapped hosts. Mailbag, Epiphany and Intake need new builds.

Unreal's limit is RAM, so it gets one day a week until a 1 TB SSD lands and the LaCie goes back to Dad.

## Oct 18

Cruise fell from 16k XP a day to 11k on slow courses, so it now picks only fast ones.

Atlin got a folded-map-and-pin icon I gave an A+. Its build in review adds Sign in with Apple and synced saves, and since then it gained price drops, alerts, map search, walk scores and open houses.

Turing can cut, join and speed up videos, make GIFs, trim sound and make Blender 3D files.

Joshua Tree was red because its checks fetched Samantha's face from my lapsed domain. The windows rework is done: four apps open at once, and a crash spares the rest. The Pi got admin accounts and a login screen.

Mailbag and Patois went into App Review, and Quotestreak got a Daily Quote, decades, hints, real difficulty and friends. Blockprint's third version shipped as one app. Dave, a friend of my dad's, bought it, my first real sale.

Everyday math and physical constants work offline. "Tell me more" works everywhere, and stock prices came back after the old quote feed locked me out. Nim went onto Android and desktop in nine languages.

I filed nine more Apple Notes into roadmaps, and cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31. I erased my plan folder from the public repo's history; only my vault has it. The Mac mini's disk kept filling, so the 5.4 GB model cache went to the LaCie.

Tripwire's fixer had never opened a pull request. Now a red main sends Claude Code to draft a fix; the first one fixed a Quotestreak test.

## Oct 19

Quotestreak got The Book, a whitepaper and a public roadmap. Quotestreak 100 shipped: a bank past 14,000 Wikiquote lines and fair play on both banks. The game is now called Coda.

I cut features nobody asked for and slowed sign-in: the live show, weekly cup, clubs, champions, hall of fame, newsletter and stream mode.

Notate's big update went live on iPhone and Apple Watch, offline from the first open.

Nimble got a live status page. Its offline pack grew to 14,000 Wikipedia articles and now passes 72 of 154 test questions, with wrong answers down from 49 to 3.

Glyphbox is Icelus now. It went from tables to symbols in one day: text charts, layers, flowcharts, sequence diagrams and a chat panel that draws a screen from a sentence.

My litigation app has its own serif and a column-and-scales logo now, and it runs in six languages. Its iPhone app launches in four seconds, down from fourteen. Today I made its repo public, after taking case details out of the code and docs. Its deadlines cite the limitation rule they follow, and reminders move with them. Grounds and witnesses work on every platform.

Thinkpond went back to App Review with App Store matches and a trademark check in its briefs. Then I decided it kind of sucked, so it's Melete now, after the Muse of thought, under a lit oil lamp on laurel green.

My portfolio at jaybulb.com is now one list of fifty projects, ranked by how far each has shipped, and I checked all 95 of its links. I also removed 33 dead heyitsmejosh.com addresses. Patois, Melete, Plaintxt and Cruise got their own jaybulb.com addresses.

I also set up a second local model, Hermes, beside Qwen, which stays the default. Qwen wrote better code in my tests; Hermes was a bit faster and better in chat, but it got git rebase wrong.

On the Pi, holding Space talks to Samantha. She opens any app Spotlight can, fetches her weather and listens until I stop. I split Joshua Tree's biggest source file, past seven thousand lines, and cut its test run from 46 minutes to 15.
