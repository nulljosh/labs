Joshua Tree hit 2.0 with twenty-six apps in protected boxes; Bookrank is live offline on iPhone.

Premium is $1, auto-trading is live, and my paper rule hit 77%.

## Oct 4

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review.

Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign; iOS is live.

## Oct 5

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a logo on Samantha's face.

Apple rejected seven apps as spam and Hikko over screenshots.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a cache fix.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi after I wrote the login handshake its chip lacked.

Samantha answers on the real Pi. Money comes from a Pi dev kit; the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote Joshua Tree's eleven-chapter book with board photos. I flashed the Pi card.

After a DNS fix it loaded heyitsmejosh.com over HTTPS; scripts don't run.

## Oct 11

I built xread, a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models. Most web apps left Supabase, so everyone resets passwords.

## Oct 13

I cut Joshua Tree's docs from fifty-six files to eleven and approved a plan to make the OS a daily driver. Turing's picker made 115 false calls; an abstain step is next.

## Oct 16

jaybulb.com had no mail records, so nothing arrived; now every address works.

Talli's Stripe webhook rejected purchases since Oct 2; fixed.

## Oct 17

All 41 apps got a new brand kit and icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I fixed ten repos. Apple's rejections came from my lapsed domain, so I swapped hosts.

## Oct 18

Cruise fell from 16k XP a day to 11k on slow courses, so it picks only fast ones.

Windows rework is done: four apps open at once, and a crash spares the rest. The Pi got admin accounts and a login screen.

Mailbag and Patois went into App Review; Quotestreak got a Daily Quote.
"Tell me more" works everywhere; stock prices came back after the old quote feed locked me out.
I cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31 and erased my plan folder from public history; only my vault has it.

## Oct 19

Quotestreak, now Coda, got a whitepaper, 14,000 Wikiquote lines.

Notate's big update went live on iPhone and Apple Watch, offline.

Glyphbox, now Icelus, draws a screen from one sentence; the App Store build waits.

My litigation app is Temida now, in six languages, with a public repo after I scrubbed case details. Screenshots bounced the iPhone app, so I redid them with a made-up case; both are back in review.

Thinkpond went back to review, but it sucked, so it's Melete.

My portfolio is one ranked list of fifty projects, and I dropped 33 of 95 dead links.

On the Pi, holding Space talks to Samantha, who opens apps and fetches weather. I split Joshua Tree's biggest source file, cutting its test run from 46 minutes to 15.

I built Hermes, a Helm clone for App Store Connect that sends nothing without my yes.

## Oct 20

Nimble passes every BC grade 10 to 12 math, science and socials exam offline at A+. New versions score low on first runs, and I'd rather show it. Confident wrong word-problem answers fell from 46 to 2.

Talli's QA caught a wrong cheque date and a dead Sign Out on iPhone; both are fixed and in review. I fixed its Alberta earnings rule to the official $350 a month; apps keep your last numbers offline.

Epiphany got a blue bulb icon and all 45 sweep problems closed.

The calling app went through Liana, Marmoset, Irisa and Oohaah, which another account owned, so it's Oohaah. Both builds are in App Review, and calls work on cellular.

I renamed Notate to Echo. Its keyboard dictates into any app by opening Echo to record. Untested on iPhone; the store still says Notate.

Cartons, the UPS tracker, went into App Review on iPhone and Mac for 99 cents.

## Oct 21

Patois uses its new name everywhere, and a streak-freeze crash is fixed. Temida's Sign in with Apple fix cleared on iPhone and Mac.

Intake was taken on the App Store, so it's Machaon. Its native apps moved off Supabase to Cloudflare, so web and phone share one account.

Epiphany's iPhone app got rebuilt; its Mac build is in review. The web and phone disagreed about my money, so I fixed my income and wiped old debt. I also scrubbed my money details from its public code and history, though one old commit stays reachable until GitHub purges it. Open issues dropped from 133 to 67.

Curvely 2.0 went into App Review on iPhone and Mac with light mode, points and shaded inequalities. Claude named it Tangent, then Pan; the store name waits.

NYC Survive is Gastown now: you command a crew, build the Steam Clock, and let drills run themselves, with no needs, raids, weapons, quests or cash. A headless bot called it unwinnable; now both bots win in twelve days. Both store builds are up, but Apple fails every screenshot, so nothing is in review.

I rebuilt the BC Garage Doors site to match bcgaragedoors.ca, cut its made-up prices and claims, and launched it on bcgd.jaybulb.com. I also built Doorstock, with parts, jobs and reorder emails, sent its Mac version to Apple, and added a Today screen for techs.

## Oct 22

Epiphany splits subscriptions by currency, so a US$200 plan stops landing in my Canadian totals. Its apps now read Fear & Greed from CNN, since my server is blocked.

Apple rejected the first Cartons and Oohaah builds; I'll read why once sign-in unthrottles.

Machaon's sign-in got the landing's plain look, Doorstock syncs shops and jobs, and garage door leads stopped going through Supabase.

My Duolingo runner is Surf now, and it's open source. A disk cleanup had wiped its build folder, so it was dead from Wednesday; I moved it out of cleanup's reach and saved the 197-day streak.

I dug up my Pre-Calc 12 masterclass, added the missing conic sections, log equations and unit circle, brought the web copy to ten units, and printed a PDF. My honest 50 to 70 percent estimate went in the study guide, and /checkup is now /doctor.

Unreal Engine kept reopening at login after three failed fixes. The culprit was a leftover startup agent from Epic's launcher; I switched it off and locked it, though I won't trust it until the next reboot.

## Oct 25

Joshua Tree's biggest source file went from 6,393 lines to 2,827 as its window manager and network commands moved out. Samantha answers on the Pi without my Mac relay, falling back to Turing's hosted Samantha when the relay is down. I stopped the relay at Joshua's request, and 5.0 waits on his board test.
