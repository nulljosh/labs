## Oct 1 to 3

Joshua Tree hit 2.0 with twenty-six apps sandboxed; Bookrank works offline on iPhone.

Premium is $1; auto-trading is live.

## Oct 4

My idea app became Hikko; iPhone and Mac are in review.

Lexly became Tonchi, now 165 courses. Healstack got a web redesign; iOS is live.

## Oct 5

Apple rejected seven apps as spam and Hikko over screenshots.

## Oct 6

The Pi 4 booted Joshua Tree at 1920x1080 after a cache fix.

## Oct 7

The Pi joined Wi-Fi once I wrote the login handshake its chip lacked. A dev kit pays for it; the OS stays free.

## Oct 8

I wrote Joshua Tree's book and flashed the Pi card.

## Oct 11

I built a digest of twenty AI accounts on X, and Plank can call AI models.

## Oct 13

Joshua Tree's landing is live with a logo on Samantha's face.

## Oct 16

Talli's Stripe webhook rejected purchases, now fixed; jaybulb.com had no mail records, now every address works.

## Oct 17

All 41 apps got a new brand kit and icon.

A security header blocked my shared tokens file, so pages fell back to Times New Roman; I fixed ten repos.

## Oct 18

Joshua Tree's windows rework is done: four apps open at once, and a crash spares the rest.

Mailbag and Patois went into App Review, Quotestreak got a Daily Quote, and stocks came back.

I cut GitHub from 62 repos to 31 and erased my plan folder from public history.

## Oct 19

Notate's big update went live on iPhone and Watch, offline. Glyphbox became Icelus; the App Store build waits.

My litigation app is Temida now; its public repo has no case details. Thinkpond became Melete.

I built Hermes, a Helm clone for App Store Connect that sends nothing without my yes.

I made my portfolio one ranked list of fifty projects and dropped 33 dead links.

## Oct 20

Nimble got A+ on every BC grade 10 to 12 math, science and socials exam offline, and its confident wrong word-problem answers fell from 46 to 2.

Epiphany got a blue bulb icon and all 45 sweep problems closed. The calling app became Oohaah after three names; both builds are in review.

Notate became Echo; the store still says Notate.

Cartons went into App Review for 99 cents.

## Oct 21

Patois uses its new name everywhere; its streak-freeze crash is fixed. Temida's Sign in with Apple fix cleared on iPhone and Mac.

Intake was taken, so it's Machaon.

The web and phone disagreed about my money, so I fixed my income and wiped old debt.

Curvely 2.0 went into App Review on iPhone and Mac as Pan.

NYC Survive is Gastown now: a crew, a Steam Clock, self-running drills, no raids or cash. A headless bot called it unwinnable; now both win in twelve days.

I rebuilt the BC Garage Doors site without its made-up prices. Doorstock, with parts, jobs and reorder emails, went to Apple.

## Oct 22

Epiphany splits subscriptions by currency, so a US$200 plan stops landing in my Canadian totals.

My Duolingo runner is Surf now. A disk cleanup had killed it since Wednesday; I moved it out of its reach and saved the 197-day streak.

Apple rejected the first Cartons and Oohaah builds; Doorstock syncs shops and jobs.

## Oct 25

Joshua Tree's main source file shrank by half, and Samantha answers on the Pi without my Mac.

I renamed Coranto to Bugle. Apple refused plain Bugle, so the store says BugleNews; both submissions were pulled.

Clock became Horula, since every clock name was taken; by night it had twelve languages.

Five Apple appeals won; Atlin's store name is back.

Melete and Machaon's Mac builds failed review on sign-in snags; both fixed and resubmitted.

## Oct 26

I cleared my Ja Gmail to zero with Mailbag: 14 filed, 4 archived.

I made Mirage, a desert platformer, and Duke beat it alone in 3,725 steps. Its iPhone, iPad and Mac apps are building at mirage.jaybulb.com.

Echo's keyboard is with Apple, untested on a phone; its horn is the icon now.

Patois's packs copy Duke's solved vocabulary, so Turkish went from 11 units to 120. I booked eight Product Hunt launches for Oct 28 to Nov 6 and appealed Cartons' iPhone spam rejection.

Patois got a full look rebuild (system font, macaw logo); Epiphany dropped its custom font.

Joshua Tree 6.0 is in CI with a sixth tuft and a three-minute Samantha cap; my board-pass SD card is flashed.

Kaggle suspended my account without a reason, so training waits on my appeal. Samantha now writes down what she can't do.

## Oct 27

Curbfind became Scrounger, since Apple wouldn't take plain Scrounge; the store still says Curbfind.

Mailbag's Mac build reads Mail.app now, so Telus and other providers work. Airwell's iPhone build was rejected for a reason I can't read.

Patois passed dark mode and phone-width checks, with 128 courses. Icelus got transitions on web and Swift; its npm and registry launches wait on me.

Noema's biggest day: a Lean proof showed Busy Beaver 2,3 is 38, and a C program counted every halting five-state Turing machine, matching the published count. Its halting times may be a new OEIS term and may answer a 2020 Aaronson question; these are candidates, not breakthroughs, and two OEIS drafts wait for me. In medicine, ClinicalTrials.gov shows 407 impossible standard deviations and 61 confidence intervals in 52 trials with a lost minus sign.

## Oct 28

Apple cleared my Sidewise spam answers except one iPad error from a dead server, so I took it off sale; it's folded into Bugle.

Bugle's iPhone and Mac builds are live; a newer Mac build is in review. Epiphany's news thumbnails shipped, and a second feed, MarketWatch, took news from 25 stories to 84.

Wordroot became Eponym, a free -nym name; the store says Wordroot until the next version.

Homeward became Sighting, my lost-pets app. Its web app got a species filter, maps and a photo-first post flow, its phone and Mac apps run on my own API now, and pet alerts for nearby posts are live.

Scrounger got category chips, saved searches, a map and price-drop badges on web, iPhone and Mac. I made commercials for both apps.

Kernel went into App Review at lunchtime, then I ran its loop until night: a storage screen, a sleep timer that fades out, a shake that adds ten minutes, and reading that follows the largest text size. It speaks French, Spanish and German now and narrates in all three; its store pages are in them too, though those go up with the next version. First-run pages offer Goodreads sign-in.

I sent 17 App Store headers to App Review and answered four spam rejections: Atlin, Horula and Oohaah.

Duke's Oxygen Not Included autopilot ate my afternoon: five colonies died by cycle 31, and the sixth, now at cycle 9, runs without algae.
